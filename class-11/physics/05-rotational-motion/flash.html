Rotational Kinematics

1Angular quantities

\(\omega=\dfrac{d\theta}{dt},\ \alpha=\dfrac{d\omega}{dt}\)
Link to linear: \(v=r\omega,\ a_t=r\alpha\).

2Equations (const α)

\(\omega=\omega_0+\alpha t,\ \omega^2=\omega_0^2+2\alpha\theta\)
Same shape as linear SUVAT.

3Moment of inertia

\[I=\textstyle\sum m r^2\]
Rotational 'mass' — depends on the axis.

4Parallel axis

\[I=I_{cm}+Md^2\]
Shift the axis a distance \(d\) off the centre.

5Perpendicular axis

\[I_z=I_x+I_y\]
Planar laminae only.

6Standard \(I\)

ring\(MR^2\)
disc\(\tfrac12MR^2\)
solid sphere\(\tfrac25MR^2\)
rod (centre)\(\tfrac1{12}ML^2\)

Rotational Dynamics

7Torque

\(\tau=rF\sin\theta=I\alpha=\dfrac{dL}{dt}\)
Rotational analogue of \(F=ma\).

8Angular momentum

\(L=I\omega=\vec r\times\vec p\)
Conserved when net external torque \(=0\).

9Conservation of \(L\)

\[I_1\omega_1=I_2\omega_2\]
Pull arms in → \(I\downarrow\Rightarrow\omega\uparrow\).

10Rotational KE

\[KE_{rot}=\tfrac12 I\omega^2\]
Plus \(\tfrac12mv^2\) when also translating.

Rolling

11Rolling condition

\(v=R\omega\)
Pure rolling = no slipping; contact point is instantaneously at rest.

12Total KE

\[KE=\tfrac12mv^2\left(1+\tfrac{I}{mR^2}\right)\]
Translation + rotation together.

13Acceleration down incline

\[a=\dfrac{g\sin\theta}{1+I/mR^2}\]
Smaller \(I/mR^2\) → faster (sphere beats ring).

14Speed at bottom

\(v=\sqrt{\dfrac{2gh}{1+I/mR^2}}\)
Independent of mass & radius.

Advanced & Traps

15Angular impulse

\[\tau\,\Delta t=\Delta L\]
The rotational impulse–momentum theorem.

16Translation + rotation

Treat the two motions separately, then combine — e.g. a rolling body = CM motion + spin about CM.

17Common traps

use the correct axis for \(I\); in pure rolling friction acts but does no work; perpendicular-axis is for laminae only.
