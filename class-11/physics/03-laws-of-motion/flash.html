Newton's Laws

1First law (inertia)

\(\vec F_{net}=0 \Rightarrow \vec v\) constant. Defines inertial frames.

2Second law

\[\vec F=\dfrac{d\vec p}{dt}=m\vec a\]
Use \(dp/dt\) for variable mass.

3Third law

\(\vec F_{AB}=-\vec F_{BA}\)
Act on different bodies — never cancel.

4Impulse

\[\vec J=\int\vec F\,dt=\Delta\vec p\]
Area under F–t; soft catch → smaller force.

5Free-body diagram

Isolate one body, draw every force, resolve on smart axes. N is a variable.

Friction & Inclines

6Static / kinetic

\(f_s\le\mu_sN,\ f_k=\mu_kN\)
Static self-adjusts; \(\mu_s>\mu_k\).

7Angle of repose

\(\tan\alpha=\mu_s\)
= angle of friction. Stays if \(\tan\theta\le\mu_s\).

8Incline acceleration

\(a=g(\sin\theta-\mu_k\cos\theta)\)
\(N=mg\cos\theta\). Up: use \(+\mu_k\).

9Connected bodies

\(a=\dfrac{F_{ext}}{\sum m}\)
Ideal string → equal T, shared a.

10Atwood

\(a=\dfrac{(m_2-m_1)g}{m_1+m_2}\)
Table+hang: \(a=\dfrac{m_2g}{m_1+m_2}\).

Circular Motion

11Centripetal

\(a_c=\dfrac{v^2}{r}=\omega^2r,\ F_c=\dfrac{mv^2}{r}\)
A name, not a new force.

12Flat road turn

\(v_{max}=\sqrt{\mu_s g r}\)
Static friction supplies \(F_c\).

13Banking

\(\tan\theta=\dfrac{v^2}{rg}\)
Frictionless design speed.

14Vertical circle

\(v_{top}=\sqrt{gr},\ v_{bot}=\sqrt{5gr}\)
Bottom tension \(=6mg\) (min loop).

15Lift / apparent weight

\(N=m(g\pm a)\)
Free fall \(\Rightarrow N=0\).

Advanced & Traps

16Pseudo-force

\(\vec F_{real}+(-m\vec a_0)=m\vec a_{rel}\)
Car bob: \(\tan\theta=a_0/g\). Only in the accelerating frame.

17Conical pendulum

\(\tan\theta=\dfrac{v^2}{rg}\)
\(T=2\pi\sqrt{L\cos\theta/g}\).

18Common traps

action–reaction on different bodies; \(N\ne mg\) in general; \(f_s\ne\mu_sN\) unless slipping; no centrifugal force in the ground frame.
