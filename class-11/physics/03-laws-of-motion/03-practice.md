## 🟢 Basic (P1–P10)

**P1.** A $6\ \mathrm{N}$ force acts on a $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ body. Find its acceleration.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a = F/m$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a = 6/3 = \mathbf{2\ m/s^2}$.
</details>

---

**P2.** Find the momentum of a $5\ \mathrm{kg}$ body moving at $4\ \mathrm{m/s}$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$p = mv$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$p = 20\ \mathbf{kg\,m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P3.** A $0.5\ \mathrm{kg}$ ball moving at $6\ \mathrm{m/s}$ is stopped in $0.1\ \mathrm{s}$. Find the average force.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$F = \Delta p/\Delta t$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$F = \dfrac{0.5 \times 6}{0.1} = \mathbf{30\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P4.** A $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ block rests on a floor with $\mu_s = 0.3$. Find the maximum static friction.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$f_{s,\max} = \mu_s mg$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$0.3(3)(10) = \mathbf{9\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P5.** A $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ block slides on a floor with $\mu_k = 0.4$. Find the friction force.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$f_k = \mu_k mg$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$0.4(2)(10) = \mathbf{8\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P6.** A $1\ \mathrm{kg}$ stone is whirled in a horizontal circle, radius $0.5\ \mathrm{m}$, at $3\ \mathrm{m/s}$. Find the tension.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$T = mv^2/r$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$T = \dfrac{1(9)}{0.5} = \mathbf{18\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P7.** A $10\ \mathrm{kg}$ block on a frictionless $30°$ incline. Find its acceleration.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a = g\sin\theta$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a = 10(0.5) = \mathbf{5\ m/s^2}$.
</details>

---

**P8.** Forces $5\ \mathrm{N}$ east and $12\ \mathrm{N}$ north act on a body. Find the net force.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Perpendicular → Pythagoras.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\sqrt{25+144} = \mathbf{13\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P9.** A $4\ \mathrm{kg}$ body changes velocity from $5$ to $9\ \mathrm{m/s}$ in $2\ \mathrm{s}$. Find the force.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$F = m\Delta v/\Delta t$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$F = 4(4/2) = \mathbf{8\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P10.** A $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ object hangs from a string in equilibrium. Find the tension.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Equilibrium → $T = mg$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$T = 2(10) = \mathbf{20\ N}$.
</details>

<a id="medium"></a>
## 🟡 Medium (P11–P25)

**P11.** A $5\ \mathrm{kg}$ block is pulled by a $30\ \mathrm{N}$ horizontal force on a floor with $\mu_k = 0.2$. Find its acceleration.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a = (F - \mu_k mg)/m$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a = (30 - 10)/5 = \mathbf{4\ m/s^2}$.
</details>

---

**P12.** A $4\ \mathrm{kg}$ block slides down a $37°$ incline with $\mu_k = 0.25$ ($\sin37°=0.6$, $\cos37°=0.8$). Find its acceleration.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a = g(\sin\theta - \mu_k\cos\theta)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a = 10(0.6 - 0.25\times0.8) = 10(0.6-0.2) = \mathbf{4\ m/s^2}$.
</details>

---

**P13.** Atwood machine: $4\ \mathrm{kg}$ and $6\ \mathrm{kg}$ over a frictionless pulley. Find the acceleration.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a = (m_2-m_1)g/(m_1+m_2)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a = 2(10)/10 = \mathbf{2\ m/s^2}$.
</details>

---

**P14.** A $50\ \mathrm{kg}$ person in a lift accelerating up at $3\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the apparent weight.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$N = m(g+a)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$N = 50(13) = \mathbf{650\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P15.** A block on a table ($m_1 = 2\ \mathrm{kg}$, frictionless) is connected over a pulley to a hanging $m_2 = 3\ \mathrm{kg}$. Find the tension.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a = m_2g/(m_1+m_2)$, then $T = m_1 a$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a = 30/5 = 6$; $T = 2(6) = \mathbf{12\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P16.** A car turns on a flat road of radius $80\ \mathrm{m}$ with $\mu_s = 0.5$. Find the maximum safe speed.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$v_{\max} = \sqrt{\mu_s g r}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\sqrt{0.5(10)(80)} = \sqrt{400} = \mathbf{20\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P17.** A ball on a string does a vertical circle of radius $0.9\ \mathrm{m}$. Find the minimum speed at the top.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$v_{\text{top}} = \sqrt{gr}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\sqrt{10 \times 0.9} = \mathbf{3\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P18.** A $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ gun fires a $30\ \mathrm{g}$ bullet at $200\ \mathrm{m/s}$. Find the recoil speed.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Momentum conservation $m_g v_g = m_b v_b$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v_g = \dfrac{0.03(200)}{3} = \mathbf{2\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P19.** Two blocks, $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ and $2\ \mathrm{kg}$, in contact on a frictionless floor, pushed by $25\ \mathrm{N}$ on the $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ block. Find the contact force.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a = F/\sum m$; contact force = $m_2 a$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a = 25/5 = 5$; contact $= 2(5) = \mathbf{10\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P20.** A block is pushed up a rough $30°$ incline, decelerating. If $\mu_k = 0.3$, find the deceleration.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Up the slope: $a = g(\sin\theta + \mu_k\cos\theta)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a = 10(0.5 + 0.3\times0.866) = 10(0.5+0.26) \approx \mathbf{7.6\ m/s^2}$.
</details>

---

**P21.** A $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ block is held against a wall by a horizontal $50\ \mathrm{N}$ force, $\mu_s = 0.5$. Does it slide?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Compare weight with $\mu_s N$ ($N = 50\ \mathrm{N}$).
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Max friction $= 0.5(50) = 25\ \mathrm{N} > mg = 20\ \mathrm{N}$ → **stays put** (does not slide).
</details>

---

**P22.** A $60\ \mathrm{kg}$ person's weighing scale reads $720\ \mathrm{N}$ in a lift. Find the acceleration and direction.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$N > mg$ → accelerating up; $N = m(g+a)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$720 = 60(10+a) \Rightarrow a = \mathbf{2\ m/s^2}$ upward.
</details>

---

**P23.** A $1\ \mathrm{kg}$ block on a $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ block (frictionless floor); interface $\mu_s = 0.4$. Max force on the **top** block to move together?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Together $a = F/3$; friction on bottom $= m_2 a \le \mu_s m_1 g$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$m_2 a \le \mu_s m_1 g \Rightarrow 2a \le 0.4(1)(10)=4 \Rightarrow a\le2$; $F_{\max}=3(2)=\mathbf{6\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P24.** A conical pendulum: string length $1\ \mathrm{m}$, bob sweeps a circle with the string at $60°$ to vertical. Find the speed of the bob.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\tan\theta = v^2/(rg)$ with $r = L\sin\theta$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$r = 1\sin60° = 0.866$; $v^2 = rg\tan60° = 0.866(10)(1.732) = 15$; $v \approx \mathbf{3.87\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P25.** A block of mass $m$ on a frictionless incline $\theta$ connected over a top pulley to hanging mass $2m$. Find the acceleration.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a = (2m - m\sin\theta)g/(3m)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a = \dfrac{(2 - \sin\theta)g}{3}$ (e.g. $\theta=30°$: $a = \dfrac{1.5(10)}{3} = \mathbf{5\ m/s^2}$).
</details>

## 🔴 Advanced (P26–P35)

**P26.** A pendulum in a car accelerating at $g$ horizontally. Find the string's angle to the vertical.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\tan\theta = a_0/g$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\tan\theta = 1 \Rightarrow \theta = \mathbf{45°}$.
</details>

---

**P27.** A $0.5\ \mathrm{kg}$ ball in a vertical circle of radius $1\ \mathrm{m}$, speed $8\ \mathrm{m/s}$ at the bottom. Find the tension at the bottom.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$T - mg = mv^2/r$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$T = 0.5(10) + \dfrac{0.5(64)}{1} = 5 + 32 = \mathbf{37\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P28.** A curve of radius $50\ \mathrm{m}$ is banked at $30°$. Find the design (frictionless) speed.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$v = \sqrt{rg\tan\theta}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v = \sqrt{50(10)\tan30°} = \sqrt{500(0.577)} = \sqrt{288.7} \approx \mathbf{17\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P29.** Sand drops onto a belt at $5\ \mathrm{kg/s}$; the belt runs at $2\ \mathrm{m/s}$. Find the force to keep it moving.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$F = v\,dm/dt$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$F = 2(5) = \mathbf{10\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P30.** A chain hangs over a rough table edge, $\mu_s = 0.5$. Find the maximum fraction that can hang.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$x = \mu_s/(1+\mu_s)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$x = 0.5/1.5 = \mathbf{1/3}$ (33%).
</details>

---

**P31.** A wedge of incline $\theta = 37°$ accelerates horizontally so a frictionless block on it doesn't slide. Find the acceleration ($\sin37°=0.6$, $\cos37°=0.8$).

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a = g\tan\theta$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a = 10(0.6/0.8) = 10(0.75) = \mathbf{7.5\ m/s^2}$.
</details>

---

**P32.** Blocks $1, 2, 3\ \mathrm{kg}$ connected in a line (1–2–3) on a frictionless floor, pulled by $18\ \mathrm{N}$ on the $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ end. Find the tension between the $2$ and $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ blocks.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a = F/6$; the $2$–$3$ string pulls the $1\ \mathrm{kg}+2\ \mathrm{kg}$ ahead of it → $T = (m_1+m_2)a$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a = 3$; $T = (1+2)(3) = \mathbf{9\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P33.** A block of mass $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ on a rough horizontal surface ($\mu_k = 0.2$) is pulled by a $20\ \mathrm{N}$ force at $30°$ above the horizontal. Find the acceleration.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$N = mg - F\sin\theta$; $a = (F\cos\theta - \mu_k N)/m$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$N = 20 - 20(0.5) = 10\ \mathrm{N}$; $f = 0.2(10) = 2$; $a = (20\times0.866 - 2)/2 = (17.3-2)/2 \approx \mathbf{7.66\ m/s^2}$.
</details>

---

**P34.** A $1\ \mathrm{kg}$ bob on a $1\ \mathrm{m}$ string swings; at the lowest point its speed is $4\ \mathrm{m/s}$. Find the tension there.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

At the lowest point $T - mg = mv^2/r$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$T = 1(10) + \dfrac{1(16)}{1} = 10 + 16 = \mathbf{26\ N}$.
</details>

---

**P35.** Two masses $m_1 = 3\ \mathrm{kg}$ (on rough table, $\mu_k=0.2$) and $m_2 = 2\ \mathrm{kg}$ (hanging) over a pulley. Find the acceleration.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a = \dfrac{m_2 g - \mu_k m_1 g}{m_1 + m_2}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a = \dfrac{20 - 0.2(3)(10)}{5} = \dfrac{20 - 6}{5} = \mathbf{2.8\ m/s^2}$.
</details>
