Work & Kinetic Energy

1Work

\(W=\vec F\cdot\vec d=Fd\cos\theta\)
Zero if \(\theta=90^\circ\); negative if opposing.

2Variable force

\[W=\int F\,dx\]
Area under F–x.

3Kinetic energy

\(K=\tfrac12mv^2=\dfrac{p^2}{2m}\)

4Work–energy theorem

\[W_{net}=\Delta K\]
Force·distance → speed, no time.

Potential Energy & Conservation

5Gravitational PE

\(U=mgh\)
Only changes matter.

6Spring PE

\(U=\tfrac12kx^2,\ F=-kx\)
Always positive.

7Conservation

\[K_1+U_1=K_2+U_2\]
Only conservative forces.

8With friction

\(\Delta(K+U)=-f\cdot d\)
Loss = \(f\times\) path length.

9Handy results

\(v=\sqrt{2gh}\); stop dist \(=\dfrac{v^2}{2\mu_kg}\).

Power & Collisions

10Power

\(P=\dfrac{W}{t}=Fv\)
Lifting: \(P=mgh/t\).

11Momentum rule

Always conserved in a collision. Start here.

12Restitution

\(e=\dfrac{v_2-v_1}{u_1-u_2}\)
Floor bounce: \(e=\sqrt{h_r/h_d}\).

13Elastic, equal mass

Exchange velocities. One at rest, 2D → separate at \(90^\circ\).

141D elastic

\(v_1=\dfrac{(m_1-m_2)u_1+2m_2u_2}{m_1+m_2}\)

Advanced & Traps

15Force from potential

\(F=-\dfrac{dU}{dx}\)
Min of U = stable → SHM.

16Vertical circle (energy)

\(v_{top}=\sqrt{gr},\ v_{bot}=\sqrt{5gr}\)
Loop from rest: \(h=\tfrac{5r}{2}\).

17Common traps

friction loss uses path length; spring PE has the ½; collisions = momentum first, KE only if elastic.
