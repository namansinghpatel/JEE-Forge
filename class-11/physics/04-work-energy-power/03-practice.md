## 🟢 Basic (P1–P10)

**P1.** A $15\ \mathrm{N}$ force moves a body $3\ \mathrm{m}$ in its own direction. Find the work.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$W = Fd\cos0°$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$W = 15(3) = \mathbf{45\ J}$.
</details>

---

**P2.** A force of $10\ \mathrm{N}$ acts at $60°$ to a $4\ \mathrm{m}$ displacement. Find the work.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$W = Fd\cos\theta$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$W = 10(4)(0.5) = \mathbf{20\ J}$.
</details>

---

**P3.** Find the kinetic energy of a $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ body at $4\ \mathrm{m/s}$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$K = \tfrac12 mv^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K = \tfrac12(3)(16) = \mathbf{24\ J}$.
</details>

---

**P4.** A $4\ \mathrm{kg}$ body is raised $3\ \mathrm{m}$. Find the gain in PE.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$U = mgh$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$U = 4(10)(3) = \mathbf{120\ J}$.
</details>

---

**P5.** A spring ($k = 100\ \mathrm{N/m}$) is compressed $0.2\ \mathrm{m}$. Find its stored energy.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$U = \tfrac12 kx^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$U = \tfrac12(100)(0.04) = \mathbf{2\ J}$.
</details>

---

**P6.** A motor does $1500\ \mathrm{J}$ of work in $5\ \mathrm{s}$. Find its power.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$P = W/t$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$P = 1500/5 = \mathbf{300\ W}$.
</details>

---

**P7.** A $6\ \mathrm{N}$ force drives a body at $4\ \mathrm{m/s}$ (same direction). Find the power.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$P = Fv$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$P = 6(4) = \mathbf{24\ W}$.
</details>

---

**P8.** A body falls from rest through $20\ \mathrm{m}$. Using energy, find its landing speed.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$mgh = \tfrac12 mv^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v = \sqrt{2(10)(20)} = \sqrt{400} = \mathbf{20\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P9.** A $1\ \mathrm{kg}$ ball at $3\ \mathrm{m/s}$ hits an identical ball at rest elastically. Find the final velocities.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Equal masses exchange velocities.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Incoming stops; struck ball moves at $\mathbf{3\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P10.** A $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ and a $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ lump moving at $5\ \mathrm{m/s}$ (same direction) and at rest collide and stick. Find the common speed.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Momentum conservation.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v = \dfrac{2(5)}{5} = \mathbf{2\ m/s}$.
</details>

<a id="medium"></a>
## 🟡 Medium (P11–P25)

**P11.** A $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ block is pulled $4\ \mathrm{m}$ along a rough floor ($\mu_k = 0.25$) by a $15\ \mathrm{N}$ force. Find the final speed from rest.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$W_{net} = \tfrac12 mv^2$; $W_{net} = (F - \mu_k mg)d$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$f = 7.5$; $W = (15-7.5)(4) = 30\ \mathrm{J}$; $v = \sqrt{2(30)/3} = \sqrt{20} \approx \mathbf{4.47\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P12.** A block slides down a frictionless incline of height $8\ \mathrm{m}$. Find its speed at the bottom.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$mgh = \tfrac12 mv^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v = \sqrt{2(10)(8)} = \sqrt{160} \approx \mathbf{12.6\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P13.** A $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ block moving at $8\ \mathrm{m/s}$ on a rough floor ($\mu_k = 0.4$) slides to rest. Find the distance.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$d = v^2/(2\mu_k g)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$d = 64/(2 \cdot 0.4 \cdot 10) = \mathbf{8\ m}$.
</details>

---

**P14.** A spring ($k = 800\ \mathrm{N/m}$) compressed $0.1\ \mathrm{m}$ launches a $0.5\ \mathrm{kg}$ block (frictionless). Find the speed.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\tfrac12 kx^2 = \tfrac12 mv^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\tfrac12(800)(0.01) = 4\ \mathrm{J}$; $v = \sqrt{2(4)/0.5} = \sqrt{16} = \mathbf{4\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P15.** A pump lifts $300\ \mathrm{kg}$ of water $20\ \mathrm{m}$ in $50\ \mathrm{s}$. Find the power.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$P = mgh/t$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$P = 300(10)(20)/50 = \mathbf{1200\ W}$.
</details>

---

**P16.** A car (mass $800\ \mathrm{kg}$) moves at constant $25\ \mathrm{m/s}$ against $600\ \mathrm{N}$ resistance. Find the power.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Constant speed → $P = Fv$ with $F$ = resistance.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$P = 600(25) = 15000\ \mathrm{W} = \mathbf{15\ kW}$.
</details>

---

**P17.** A $4\ \mathrm{kg}$ ball at $3\ \mathrm{m/s}$ hits a $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ ball at rest and they stick. Find the common velocity and KE lost.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Momentum for $v$; then $\Delta KE$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v = 12/6 = 2\ \mathrm{m/s}$; $KE_i = 18$, $KE_f = 12$; lost $= \mathbf{6\ J}$.
</details>

---

**P18.** A ball dropped from $2\ \mathrm{m}$ rebounds to $1.28\ \mathrm{m}$. Find $e$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$e = \sqrt{h_r/h_d}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$e = \sqrt{1.28/2} = \sqrt{0.64} = \mathbf{0.8}$.
</details>

---

**P19.** A pendulum of length $2\ \mathrm{m}$ is released from the horizontal. Find the speed at the lowest point.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$mgL = \tfrac12 mv^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v = \sqrt{2(10)(2)} = \sqrt{40} \approx \mathbf{6.32\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P20.** A $1\ \mathrm{kg}$ block at $5\ \mathrm{m/s}$ on a frictionless floor hits a spring ($k = 250\ \mathrm{N/m}$). Find the maximum compression.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\tfrac12 mv^2 = \tfrac12 kx^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$x = v\sqrt{m/k} = 5\sqrt{1/250} = 5(0.0632) \approx \mathbf{0.316\ m}$.
</details>

---

**P21.** An elastic head-on collision: $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ at $4\ \mathrm{m/s}$ hits $1\ \mathrm{kg}$ at rest. Find both final velocities.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

1D elastic formulas with $u_2 = 0$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v_1 = \dfrac{(3-1)4}{4} = 2$; $v_2 = \dfrac{2(3)(4)}{4} = 6$. So $\mathbf{2\ m/s}$ and $\mathbf{6\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P22.** A $20\ \mathrm{g}$ bullet at $150\ \mathrm{m/s}$ embeds in a $980\ \mathrm{g}$ block on a frictionless floor. Find the common speed.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Momentum conservation.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v = \dfrac{0.02(150)}{1.0} = \mathbf{3\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P23.** A $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ block slides down a rough incline (height $4\ \mathrm{m}$, length $8\ \mathrm{m}$, $\mu_k = 0.2$). Find the bottom speed.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$mgh - \mu_k mg\cos\theta\, L = \tfrac12 mv^2$; $\cos\theta = \sqrt{L^2-h^2}/L$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\cos\theta = \sqrt{48}/8 = 0.866$; $mgh = 80$; friction $= 0.2(2)(10)(0.866)(8) = 27.7\ \mathrm{J}$; $v = \sqrt{2(52.3)/2} \approx \mathbf{7.2\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P24.** A body of mass $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ has PE $U = 3x^2\ \mathrm{J}$. Find the force at $x = 2\ \mathrm{m}$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$F = -dU/dx$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$F = -6x = \mathbf{-12\ N}$ at $x=2$ (toward the origin).
</details>

---

**P25.** A $0.5\ \mathrm{kg}$ ball on a string does a vertical circle of radius $0.8\ \mathrm{m}$. Find the minimum speed at the top.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$v_{top} = \sqrt{gr}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v = \sqrt{10(0.8)} = \sqrt{8} \approx \mathbf{2.83\ m/s}$.
</details>

## 🔴 Advanced (P26–P35)

**P26.** A force $F = 2x + 3\ \mathrm{N}$ acts from $x = 0$ to $x = 2\ \mathrm{m}$. Find the work.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$W = \int F\,dx$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$W = \int_0^2 (2x+3)\,dx = [x^2 + 3x]_0^2 = 4 + 6 = \mathbf{10\ J}$.
</details>

---

**P27.** A particle's PE is $U(x) = x^3 - 3x\ \mathrm{J}$. Find the equilibrium positions and their stability.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$F = -dU/dx = 0$; check sign of $d^2U/dx^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$dU/dx = 3x^2 - 3 = 0 \Rightarrow x = \pm1$. $d^2U/dx^2 = 6x$: $x=1$ stable (min), $x=-1$ unstable (max).
</details>

---

**P28.** A ball on a string does a vertical circle of radius $1.25\ \mathrm{m}$. Find the minimum speed at the bottom to complete the loop.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$v_{bot} = \sqrt{5gr}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v = \sqrt{5(10)(1.25)} = \sqrt{62.5} \approx \mathbf{7.9\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P29.** A $1\ \mathrm{kg}$ block pushed against a spring ($k = 400\ \mathrm{N/m}$), compression $0.3\ \mathrm{m}$, on a rough floor ($\mu_k = 0.25$), is released. Find the speed as it leaves the spring.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\tfrac12 kx^2 = \tfrac12 mv^2 + \mu_k mg\,x$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Spring PE $= \tfrac12(400)(0.09) = 18\ \mathrm{J}$; friction $= 0.25(1)(10)(0.3) = 0.75\ \mathrm{J}$; $v = \sqrt{2(17.25)/1} \approx \mathbf{5.87\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P30.** A block is released from the top of a frictionless sphere of radius $R$. At what angle from the top does it leave the surface?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Leaves when $N = 0$: $\cos\theta = 2/3$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\theta = \cos^{-1}(2/3) \approx \mathbf{48.2°}$ from the top.
</details>

---

**P31.** Equal masses, elastic collision, one at rest. If the incoming ball ($6\ \mathrm{m/s}$) goes off at $37°$, find both final speeds.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

They separate at $90°$; $v_1 = u\cos37°$, $v_2 = u\sin37°$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v_1 = 6(0.8) = 4.8\ \mathrm{m/s}$; $v_2 = 6(0.6) = \mathbf{3.6\ m/s}$ (at $53°$).
</details>

---

**P32.** A $5\ \mathrm{kg}$ body at $4\ \mathrm{m/s}$ collides with a $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ body at rest; $e = 0.5$. Find both final velocities.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Momentum + restitution ($v_2 - v_1 = e\,u_1$).
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$20 = 5v_1 + 3v_2$; $v_2 - v_1 = 2$. Solve: $v_1 = 1.75$, $v_2 = 3.75\ \mathrm{m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P33.** A chain of mass $m$, length $L$, starts fully on a frictionless table and slides off. Find its speed as the last link leaves (use energy; CM falls $L/2$).

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$mg(L/2) = \tfrac12 mv^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$v = \sqrt{gL}$.
</details>

---

**P34.** A $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ block on a rough surface ($\mu_k = 0.2$) is pushed by a horizontal $20\ \mathrm{N}$ force over $5\ \mathrm{m}$. Find the net work and final KE (from rest).

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$W_{net} = (F - \mu_k mg)d = \Delta KE$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$f = 0.2(2)(10) = 4$; $W = (20-4)(5) = \mathbf{80\ J}$ = final KE.
</details>

---

**P35.** A loop-the-loop track: find the minimum release height (from rest, smooth track) for a bead to complete a vertical circle of radius $r = 2\ \mathrm{m}$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$h_{min} = \tfrac{5r}{2}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$h = \tfrac{5(2)}{2} = \mathbf{5\ m}$.
</details>
