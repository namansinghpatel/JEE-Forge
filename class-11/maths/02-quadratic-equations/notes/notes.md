## 🎯 Quick summary (the whole topic in a nutshell)

A quadratic equation is any equation of the form $ax^2+bx+c=0$ — the highest power is 2. The
whole topic is about its two **roots**: finding them, describing them, and controlling them with
parameters.

**The five things to really understand:**

1. **The quadratic formula always works:** $x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$. Factorise when
   it's easy; otherwise use the formula.
2. **The discriminant $D = b^2-4ac$ tells you the roots' nature without solving.** $D>0$: two real
   and different; $D=0$: one repeated; $D<0$: complex (no real roots).
3. **You rarely need the actual roots.** The sum $\alpha+\beta = -b/a$ and product $\alpha\beta = c/a$
   (Vieta's relations) let you compute almost anything about the roots directly — this is the single
   most useful idea for JEE.
4. **A quadratic is a parabola.** $a>0$ opens up (has a minimum), $a<0$ opens down (has a maximum);
   the turning point (vertex) is at $x = -b/2a$ and the extreme value is $-D/4a$. The graph makes the
   "nature" and "sign" questions obvious.
5. **Controlling roots with conditions.** "Both roots positive", "a number lies between the roots",
   "roots real" — these are solved by combining three checks: the discriminant, the sum, and the
   product (or the sign of $f$ at a point).
