## 🟢 Basic

<a id="q1"></a>
### Q1
Solve $x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0$.

<details><summary>💡 Hint (approach)</summary>

Factorise: find two numbers that multiply to $12$ and add to $7$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer & Solution</summary>

$(x-3)(x-4)=0 \Rightarrow x = \mathbf{3,\ 4}$.
</details>

### Q2
Find the discriminant of $3x^2 - 2x + 5 = 0$ and the nature of its roots.

<details><summary>💡 Hint (approach)</summary>

$D = b^2 - 4ac$; its sign decides the nature.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer & Solution</summary>

$D = 4 - 60 = -56 < 0 \Rightarrow$ **two complex conjugate roots**.
</details>

### Q11

<details><summary>💡 Hint (approach)</summary>

<a id="q12"></a>
### Q12
If $\alpha,\beta$ are roots of $3x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0$, find $\alpha^2 + \beta^2$.

<details><summary>💡 Hint (approach)</summary>

$\alpha^2+\beta^2 = S^2 - 2P$ with $S = -b/a$, $P = c/a$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer & Solution</summary>

$S = \tfrac43$, $P = \tfrac13$; $\left(\tfrac43\right)^2 - \tfrac23 = \tfrac{16}{9} - \tfrac69 = \mathbf{\tfrac{10}{9}}$.
</details>

Find the values of $k$ for which $x^2 + 2(k-1)x + (k+5) = 0$ has real roots.

<details><summary>💡 Hint (approach)</summary>

<a id="q26"></a>
### Q26
Find all $a$ for which both roots of $x^2 - (a-3)x + a = 0$ are positive.

<details><summary>💡 Hint (approach)</summary>

Three conditions: $D \geq 0$, sum $>0$, product $>0$. Intersect them.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer & Solution</summary>

product $a>0$; sum $(a-3)>0 \Rightarrow a>3$; $D=(a-3)^2-4a\geq0 \Rightarrow (a-1)(a-9)\geq0 \Rightarrow a\leq1$ or $a\geq9$. Intersect with $a>3$: $\mathbf{a \geq 9}$.
</details>

<a id="q27"></a>
### Q27
If $\alpha,\beta$ are roots of $x^2 - x - 1 = 0$, find $\alpha^5 + \beta^5$.

<details><summary>💡 Hint (approach)</summary>

Use the recurrence $p_n = S\,p_{n-1} - P\,p_{n-2}$ with $p_0=2$, $p_1=S$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer & Solution</summary>

$S=1,P=-1$: $p_2=3,\ p_3=4,\ p_4=7,\ p_5=\mathbf{11}$. (These are Lucas numbers.)
</details>

### Q28
Find the range of $y = \dfrac{x^2 - 3x + 4}{x^2 + 3x + 4}$ for real $x$.

<details><summary>💡 Hint (approach)</summary>

Cross-multiply, collect as a quadratic in $x$, impose $D \geq 0$ in $y$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer & Solution</summary>

$(y-1)x^2 + 3(y+1)x + 4(y-1)=0$; $D\geq0 \Rightarrow 9(y+1)^2 - 16(y-1)^2 \geq 0 \Rightarrow (7-y)(7y-1)\geq0 \Rightarrow \mathbf{y \in [\tfrac17, 7]}$.
</details>

<a id="q30"></a>
### Q30
If $x^2 + px + q = 0$ and $x^2 + qx + p = 0$ ($p \neq q$) have a common root, find the relation between $p$ and $q$.

<details><summary>💡 Hint (approach)</summary>

Subtract the equations to eliminate $x^2$ and find the common root.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer & Solution</summary>

$(p-q)(x-1)=0 \Rightarrow x=1$; substitute: $1+p+q=0 \Rightarrow p+q = -1$.
</details>

<a id="q31"></a>
### Q31
If $\alpha,\beta$ are roots of $x^2 - 3x + 1 = 0$, form the quadratic with roots $\alpha^4,\beta^4$.

<details><summary>💡 Hint (approach)</summary>

Climb powers: $\alpha^2+\beta^2 = S^2-2P$, then $\alpha^4+\beta^4 = (\alpha^2+\beta^2)^2 - 2(\alpha\beta)^2$. Product $=(\alpha\beta)^4$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer & Solution</summary>

$S=3,P=1$: $\alpha^2+\beta^2=7$; $\alpha^4+\beta^4 = 49-2 = 47$; product $=1$. $\Rightarrow x^2 - 47x + 1 = 0$.
</details>

If $\alpha,\beta$ are roots of $x^2 - 5x + 4 = 0$, form the quadratic with roots $\dfrac\alpha\beta, \dfrac\beta\alpha$.

New sum $= \dfrac{\alpha^2+\beta^2}{\alpha\beta}$, new product $=1$. (Roots are $1,4$.)

<a id="q35"></a>
### Q35
The roots of $x^2 - bx + c = 0$ are two consecutive **even** integers. Show that $b^2 - 4c = 4$.

<details><summary>💡 Hint (approach)</summary>

Let the roots be $n$ and $n+2$; express $b,c$ via sum and product, then compute $b^2 - 4c$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer & Solution</summary>

$b = 2n+2$, $c = n(n+2)$; $b^2 - 4c = (2n+2)^2 - 4n(n+2) = 4n^2+8n+4 - 4n^2-8n = \mathbf{4}$.
</details>
