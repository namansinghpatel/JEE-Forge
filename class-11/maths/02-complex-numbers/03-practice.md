## 🟢 Basic (P1–P10)

**P1.** Simplify $i^{15}$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Reduce exponent mod 4.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$i^{15}=i^3=\mathbf{-i}$.
</details>

---

**P2.** $(4 - i) - (2 + 3i)$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Subtract parts.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$2 - 4i$.
</details>

---

**P3.** $(1 + i)(1 - i)$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$(a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$1 - i^2 = \mathbf{2}$.
</details>

---

**P4.** Modulus of $z = 5 + 12i$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\sqrt{25+144}=\mathbf{13}$.
</details>

---

**P5.** Conjugate of $z = -2 + 7i$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Flip sign of imaginary part.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$-2 - 7i$.
</details>

---

**P6.** $\sqrt{-25}$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Factor out $i$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$5i$.
</details>

---

**P7.** $z\bar z$ for $z = 1 + 2i$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$|z|^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\mathbf{5}$.
</details>

---

**P8.** Argument of $z = i$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

On the positive imaginary axis.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
</details>

---

**P9.** $1 + \omega + \omega^2 = ?$

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Cube roots of unity.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\mathbf{0}$.
</details>

---

**P10.** Modulus of $z = -1 - i$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\sqrt{1+1}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\sqrt2$.
</details>

<a id="medium"></a>
## 🟡 Medium (P11–P25)

**P11.** Express $\dfrac{1}{1 - i}$ as $a + ib$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Multiply by conjugate.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\dfrac{1+i}{2}=\dfrac12+\dfrac12 i$.
</details>

---

**P12.** Argument of $z = -\sqrt3 + i$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

2nd quadrant.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$150°$ ($5\pi/6$).
</details>

---

**P13.** $(1 - i)^4$ using De Moivre?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$r=\sqrt2$, $\theta=-45°$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$(\sqrt2)^4\text{cis}(-180°)=4(-1)=\mathbf{-4}$.
</details>

---

**P14.** $\omega^{25} + \omega^{50}$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Reduce mod 3.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$25\equiv1,50\equiv2$: $\omega+\omega^2=\mathbf{-1}$.
</details>

---

**P15.** Modulus and argument of $z = 1 + i\sqrt3$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$r=2$, 1st quadrant.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$|z|=2$, $\arg=60°$.
</details>

---

**P16.** $\left|\dfrac{2+i}{1-2i}\right|$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Ratio of moduli.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\dfrac{\sqrt5}{\sqrt5}=\mathbf{1}$.
</details>

---

**P17.** Solve $z^2 = -1$ for all $z$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Square roots of $-1$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$z=\pm i$.
</details>

---

**P18.** Real $x$: $(x + 2i)(x - 2i) = 13$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$x^2+4=13$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$x=\pm3$.
</details>

---

**P19.** Polar form of $z = -4$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Negative real axis.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$4(\cos180°+i\sin180°)$.
</details>

---

**P20.** $(\cos15°+i\sin15°)^{12}$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Angle ×12.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\cos180°+i\sin180°=\mathbf{-1}$.
</details>

---

**P21.** Solve $z^2 - z + 1 = 0$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Quadratic formula with $D<0$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$z=\dfrac{1\pm i\sqrt3}{2}$ (these are $-\omega,-\omega^2$).
</details>

---

**P22.** Locus of $|z - 3i| = 2$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$|z-z_0|=r$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Circle, centre $(0,3)$, radius $2$.
</details>

---

**P23.** Find the square roots of $-15 + 8i$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a^2-b^2=-15$, $2ab=8$, $a^2+b^2=17$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a^2=1,b^2=16$: $z=\pm(1+4i)$.
</details>

---

**P24.** $\operatorname{Re}(z)$ and $\operatorname{Im}(z)$ for $z = (2 - i)^2$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Expand.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$4-4i+i^2=3-4i$: Re $=3$, Im $=-4$.
</details>

---

**P25.** If $|z| = 1$, what is $z\bar z$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$z\bar z=|z|^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\mathbf{1}$.
</details>

<a id="advanced"></a>
## 🔴 Advanced (P26–P35)

**P26.** Find all cube roots of unity in $a + ib$ form.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$z^3=1$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$1,\ \dfrac{-1+i\sqrt3}{2},\ \dfrac{-1-i\sqrt3}{2}$.
</details>

---

**P27.** Find the fourth roots of $-16$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$-16=16e^{i\pi}$; roots $2e^{i(\pi+2\pi k)/4}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$2\text{cis}45°,2\text{cis}135°,2\text{cis}225°,2\text{cis}315°$ (i.e. $\sqrt2(\pm1\pm i)$).
</details>

---

**P28.** Rotate $z = 2$ by $60°$ about the origin.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Multiply by $e^{i60°}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$2(\cos60°+i\sin60°)=1+i\sqrt3$.
</details>

---

**P29.** Sum $1+\omega+\omega^2+\dots+\omega^{3n-1}$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Groups of 3.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\mathbf{0}$.
</details>

---

**P30.** If $z = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta$, find $z^5 + \dfrac{1}{z^5}$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$z^n+z^{-n}=2\cos n\theta$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$2\cos5\theta$.
</details>

---

**P31.** Locus of $|z - 1| = |z - i|$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Perpendicular bisector of the segment joining $1$ and $i$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

The line $y = x$.
</details>

---

**P32.** Use De Moivre to express $\sin3\theta$ in terms of $\sin\theta$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Imaginary part of $(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^3$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\sin3\theta=3\sin\theta-4\sin^3\theta$.
</details>

---

**P33.** If $1,\alpha,\alpha^2,\dots,\alpha^{n-1}$ are the $n$th roots of unity, find their product.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Product $=(-1)^{n-1}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$(-1)^{n-1}$.
</details>

---

**P34.** Find $\left|\,(1+i)^{10}\,\right|$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$|z^n|=|z|^n$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$(\sqrt2)^{10}=\mathbf{32}$.
</details>

---

**P35.** For which $z$ is $\dfrac{z-1}{z+1}$ purely imaginary?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Purely imaginary ⇔ its real part is 0 ⇔ $|z|=1$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

On the unit circle $|z|=1$ (excluding $z=\pm1$).
</details>
