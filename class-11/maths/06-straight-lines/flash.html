Slope & Angle

1Slope

\(m=\tfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}=\tan\theta\)

2From general form

\(m=-\tfrac ab\) for \(ax+by+c=0\)

3Angle between

\(\tan\theta=\left|\tfrac{m_1-m_2}{1+m_1m_2}\right|\)

4∥ & ⟂

\(m_1=m_2\) parallel · \(m_1m_2=-1\) perpendicular.

Forms of a Line

5Slope–intercept

\(y=mx+c\)

6Point–slope

\(y-y_1=m(x-x_1)\)

7Intercept

\(\tfrac xa+\tfrac yb=1\)

8Normal

\(x\cos\alpha+y\sin\alpha=p\)

Distance & Points

9Point to line

\(d=\tfrac{|ax_1+by_1+c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}\)

10Between parallels

\(d=\tfrac{|c_1-c_2|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}\)
make a, b match first.

11Area / collinear

\(\tfrac12|x_1(y_2-y_3)+\dots|\)
= 0 ⟺ collinear.

12Foot & image

one ratio \(t=-\tfrac{ax_1+by_1+c}{a^2+b^2}\): foot +at, image +2at.

Advanced Tools

13Family of lines

\(L_1+\lambda L_2=0\)
any line through an intersection.

14Angle bisectors

\(\tfrac{L_1}{\sqrt{a_1^2+b_1^2}}=\pm\tfrac{L_2}{\sqrt{a_2^2+b_2^2}}\)

15Concurrency

3×3 determinant of \((a,b,c)\) rows \(=0\).

16Pair of lines

\(ax^2+2hxy+by^2=0\)
⟂ iff \(a+b=0\); slopes sum \(-2h/b\), product \(a/b\).
