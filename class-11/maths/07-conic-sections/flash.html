Foundations

1Eccentricity picks the shape

\(e=0\)circle
\(0ellipse
\(e=1\)parabola
\(e>1\)hyperbola

2Focus–directrix

\(\dfrac{PS}{PM}=e\)
One rule generates all four conics.

3Read the type

No \(xy\): \(A=C\) circle; one square → parabola; same signs → ellipse; opposite → hyperbola.

Circle & Parabola

4Circle

\((x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2\)
General: centre \((-g,-f)\), \(r=\sqrt{g^2+f^2-c}\).

5Circle tangent

\(xx_1+yy_1=r^2\)
Line tangent when distance from centre \(=r\).

6Parabola \(y^2=4ax\)

Focus \((a,0)\), directrix \(x=-a\), latus rectum \(4a\).

7Parametric point

\((at^2,\,2at)\)
Focal chord: \(t_1t_2=-1\).

Ellipse & Hyperbola

8Ellipse

\(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1\)
\(b^2=a^2(1-e^2)\); foci \((\pm ae,0)\); \(PS_1+PS_2=2a\).

9Hyperbola

\(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1\)
\(b^2=a^2(e^2-1)\); asymptotes \(y=\pm\tfrac{b}{a}x\); \(|PS_1-PS_2|=2a\).

10Latus rectum

\(\dfrac{2b^2}{a}\)
Same for ellipse & hyperbola.

11Tangent conditions

circle\(c^2=r^2(1+m^2)\)
parabola\(c=a/m\)
ellipse\(c^2=a^2m^2+b^2\)
hyperbola\(c^2=a^2m^2-b^2\)

12Director circle

\(x^2+y^2=a^2+b^2\)
Locus of points with perpendicular tangents (ellipse).

Traps

13Which axis is major?

Compare denominators — the larger one sets the major/transverse axis direction.

14Centre not at origin

Complete the square first, then use the standard formulas about the new centre.

15Sign flip

\(+b^2\) (ellipse) vs \(-b^2\) (hyperbola) in both \(b^2\)-relation and tangent condition.

16One tangent rule

\(T=0\): tangent at a point and chord of contact from outside.
