## 🟢 Basic (P1–P10)

**P1.** Find the centre and radius of $x^2+y^2-6x-8y=0$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Centre $=(-g,-f)$, radius $=\sqrt{g^2+f^2-c}$. Here $c=0$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$g=-3,f=-4,c=0$. Centre $(3,4)$, radius $\sqrt{9+16}=\mathbf{5}$.
</details>

---

**P2.** Write the equation of the circle centred at the origin with radius $7$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Standard form $x^2+y^2=r^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$x^2+y^2=49$.
</details>

---

**P3.** For $y^2=20x$, find the focus and directrix.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$4a=20$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a=5$. Focus $(5,0)$, directrix $x=-5$.
</details>

---

**P4.** State the direction of opening and the value of $a$ for $x^2=16y$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$x^2$ positive → opens up; $4a=16$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Opens **up**, $a=4$ (focus $(0,4)$, directrix $y=-4$).
</details>

---

**P5.** Find the eccentricity of $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$e=\sqrt{1-b^2/a^2}$ with $a^2=9,b^2=4$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$e=\sqrt{1-\tfrac49}=\sqrt{\tfrac59}=\dfrac{\sqrt5}{3}$.
</details>

---

**P6.** Find the latus rectum of $\dfrac{x^2}{36}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\dfrac{2b^2}{a}$, $a=6,b=4$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\dfrac{2\cdot16}{6}=\dfrac{32}{6}=\mathbf{\tfrac{16}{3}}$.
</details>

---

**P7.** Find $e$ and the foci of $\dfrac{x^2}{9}-\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Hyperbola: $e=\sqrt{1+b^2/a^2}$, foci $(\pm ae,0)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$e=\sqrt{1+\tfrac{16}{9}}=\tfrac53$; foci $(\pm3\cdot\tfrac53,0)=(\pm5,0)$.
</details>

---

**P8.** Write the asymptotes of $\dfrac{x^2}{25}-\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$y=\pm\dfrac{b}{a}x$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$y=\pm\dfrac{2}{5}x$.
</details>

---

**P9.** Identify the conic: $4x^2+4y^2=1$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Equal coefficients of $x^2$ and $y^2$ → circle.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$x^2+y^2=\tfrac14$ → **circle**, radius $\tfrac12$.
</details>

---

**P10.** What type of conic is $x^2-y^2=9$, and what is its eccentricity?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Opposite signs → hyperbola; $a=b$ here.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Rectangular **hyperbola** ($a=b=3$), $e=\sqrt2$.
</details>

<a id="medium"></a>
## 🟡 Medium (P11–P25)

**P11.** Find the circle through $(0,0)$, $(a,0)$ and $(0,b)$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Through origin → $c=0$; substitute the other two points.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$x^2+y^2-ax-by=0$ (centre $(\tfrac a2,\tfrac b2)$).
</details>

---

**P12.** Find the circle with centre $(-1,2)$ tangent to the $y$-axis.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Radius = distance from centre to the $y$-axis $=|x\text{-coordinate}|$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$r=1$; $(x+1)^2+(y-2)^2=1$.
</details>

---

**P13.** Find the parabola with vertex $(0,0)$ and directrix $y=3$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Directrix above vertex → opens **down**, $a=3$, form $x^2=-4ay$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$x^2=-12y$.
</details>

---

**P14.** A parabola $y^2=4ax$ passes through $(3,6)$. Find $a$ and the latus rectum.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Substitute the point to get $4a$ in one step.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$36=4a(3)\Rightarrow 4a=12$, so $a=3$, latus rectum $=12$.
</details>

---

**P15.** Find the ellipse with vertices $(\pm6,0)$ and foci $(\pm4,0)$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a=6$, $c=4$, $b^2=a^2-c^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$b^2=36-16=20$; $\dfrac{x^2}{36}+\dfrac{y^2}{20}=1$.
</details>

---

**P16.** Find the eccentricity of an ellipse whose minor axis equals the distance between the foci.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Minor axis $=2b$, distance between foci $=2c=2ae$; set equal → relate $b$ and $ae$, then use $b^2=a^2(1-e^2)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$2b=2ae\Rightarrow b=ae\Rightarrow b^2=a^2e^2$. Also $b^2=a^2(1-e^2)$, so $e^2=1-e^2\Rightarrow e=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$.
</details>

---

**P17.** Find the hyperbola with vertices $(\pm2,0)$ and eccentricity $2$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a=2$, $b^2=a^2(e^2-1)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$b^2=4(4-1)=12$; $\dfrac{x^2}{4}-\dfrac{y^2}{12}=1$.
</details>

---

**P18.** Find the tangent to $x^2+y^2=13$ at $(2,3)$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$xx_1+yy_1=r^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$2x+3y=13$.
</details>

---

**P19.** For $y^2=12x$, find the focal distance of the point $(3,6)$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Focal distance $=x+a$; $4a=12$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a=3$; focal distance $=3+3=6$.
</details>

---

**P20.** Find the directrices of $\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{7}=1$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$e=\sqrt{1-b^2/a^2}$, directrices $x=\pm a/e$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$e=\sqrt{1-\tfrac{7}{16}}=\tfrac34$; $x=\pm\dfrac{4}{3/4}=\pm\dfrac{16}{3}$.
</details>

---

**P21.** For what $c$ is $y=3x+c$ tangent to $y^2=12x$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$c=a/m$ with $4a=12$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a=3,m=3\Rightarrow c=\dfrac{3}{3}=1$.
</details>

---

**P22.** Find the circle on the join of $(2,-3)$ and $(-4,5)$ as a diameter.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Diameter form $(x-x_1)(x-x_2)+(y-y_1)(y-y_2)=0$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$(x-2)(x+4)+(y+3)(y-5)=0 \Rightarrow x^2+y^2+2x-2y-23=0$.
</details>

---

**P23.** A point on $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$ is $4$ units from one focus. How far is it from the other focus?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$PS_1+PS_2=2a$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$2a=10$, so other distance $=10-4=6$.
</details>

---

**P24.** Find the eccentricity of the hyperbola $\dfrac{x^2}{9}-\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$a=b$ → rectangular hyperbola.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$e=\sqrt{1+1}=\sqrt2$.
</details>

---

**P25.** The line $x+y=5$ touches a circle centred at the origin. Find the radius.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Tangent ⇒ radius = perpendicular distance from centre to the line.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$r=\dfrac{|0+0-5|}{\sqrt{1^2+1^2}}=\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}$.
</details>

<a id="advanced"></a>
## 🔴 Advanced (P26–P35)

**P26.** Find the circle through $(5,7)$, $(6,6)$ and $(2,-2)$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

General form; subtract equations pairwise to eliminate $c$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$x^2+y^2-4x-6y-12=0$ (centre $(2,3)$, radius $5$).
</details>

---

**P27.** For what $c$ is $y=2x+c$ tangent to $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$c^2=a^2m^2+b^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$c^2=9(4)+4=40\Rightarrow c=\pm2\sqrt{10}$.
</details>

---

**P28.** A focal chord of $y^2=8x$ has one end at $t=1$. Find the other end and the chord length.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Points $(at^2,2at)$ with $a=2$; focal chord ⇒ $t_1t_2=-1$; length $=a(t+1/t)^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$t_2=-1$. Ends $(2,4)$ and $(2,-4)$. Length $=2(1+1)^2=8$ (this is the latus rectum, since $t=1$).
</details>

---

**P29.** Find the eccentricity of an ellipse whose latus rectum equals one-third of the major axis.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\dfrac{2b^2}{a}=\dfrac13(2a)$; reduce to $b^2/a^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\dfrac{2b^2}{a}=\dfrac{2a}{3}\Rightarrow \dfrac{b^2}{a^2}=\dfrac13$; $e=\sqrt{1-\tfrac13}=\sqrt{\tfrac23}=\dfrac{\sqrt6}{3}$.
</details>

---

**P30.** Find the hyperbola (centre origin, transverse axis along $x$) with asymptotes $y=\pm\tfrac12 x$ passing through $(4,0)$... then check: is $(4,0)$ consistent? Instead take it through $(\sqrt{20},1)$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$b/a=\tfrac12$ → $a=2b$; write $\dfrac{x^2}{4b^2}-\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ and substitute the point.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\dfrac{20}{4b^2}-\dfrac{1}{b^2}=\dfrac{5}{b^2}-\dfrac{1}{b^2}=\dfrac{4}{b^2}=1\Rightarrow b^2=4,\ a^2=16$. Equation $\dfrac{x^2}{16}-\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$.
</details>

---

**P31.** Reduce $4x^2+9y^2+16x-18y-11=0$ to standard form; state type, centre and $e$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Complete the square in $x$ and $y$ separately.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$4(x+2)^2+9(y-1)^2=36\Rightarrow \dfrac{(x+2)^2}{9}+\dfrac{(y-1)^2}{4}=1$. Ellipse, centre $(-2,1)$, $a=3,b=2$, $e=\dfrac{\sqrt5}{3}$.
</details>

---

**P32.** Find the chord of contact from $(4,5)$ to $x^2+y^2=10$, and the tangent length.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Chord of contact $xx_1+yy_1=r^2$; tangent length $\sqrt{S_1}=\sqrt{x_1^2+y_1^2-r^2}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Chord of contact $4x+5y=10$; tangent length $\sqrt{16+25-10}=\sqrt{31}$.
</details>

---

**P33.** Find the locus of points from which the tangents to $\dfrac{x^2}{9}-\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$... use the director circle $x^2+y^2=a^2-b^2$. Where is it real?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

For a hyperbola the director circle is $x^2+y^2=a^2-b^2$; it is a real circle only if $a^2>b^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$x^2+y^2=9-4=5$ — a real circle of radius $\sqrt5$ (real because $a^2>b^2$).
</details>

---

**P34.** An ellipse has foci $(\pm3,0)$ and passes through $(0,4)$. Find its equation.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Use $PS_1+PS_2=2a$ at $(0,4)$, then $b^2=a^2-c^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Each focal distance $=\sqrt{9+16}=5$, so $2a=10,a=5$; $b^2=25-9=16$. $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$.
</details>

---

**P35.** Find the equation of the parabola with focus $(3,0)$ and vertex $(1,0)$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Axis is the $x$-axis; vertex not at origin, so shift. Distance vertex→focus $=a$; opens toward the focus. Use $(y-k)^2=4a(x-h)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$a=2$, opens right from vertex $(1,0)$: $(y-0)^2=4(2)(x-1)\Rightarrow y^2=8(x-1)$.
</details>
