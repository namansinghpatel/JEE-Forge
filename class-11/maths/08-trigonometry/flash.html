Fundamentals

1Pythagorean

\(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1\)

2Cousins

\(1+\tan^2=\sec^2,\ 1+\cot^2=\csc^2\)

3Signs (ASTC)

Q1 all+ · Q2 sin+ · Q3 tan+ · Q4 cos+.

4Ranges & periods

sin,cos ∈[−1,1], period 2π; tan period π.

Compound Angles

5sin(A±B)

\(\sin A\cos B\pm\cos A\sin B\)

6cos(A±B)

\(\cos A\cos B\mp\sin A\sin B\)

7tan(A±B)

\(\tfrac{\tan A\pm\tan B}{1\mp\tan A\tan B}\)

8Use

non-standard angles: sin75°=sin(45°+30°).

Multiple & Sum↔Product

9Double angle

\(\sin2A=2\sin A\cos A\)

10cos 2A

\(1-2\sin^2A=2\cos^2A-1\)

11Triple

\(\cos3A=4\cos^3A-3\cos A\)

12Sum→product

\(\sin C+\sin D=2\sin\tfrac{C+D}2\cos\tfrac{C-D}2\)

Equations & Range

13General: sin

\(\theta=n\pi+(-1)^n\alpha\)

14General: cos

\(\theta=2n\pi\pm\alpha\)

15General: tan

\(\theta=n\pi+\alpha\)

16R-form range

\(a\cos\theta+b\sin\theta\in[-R,R]\)
R=√(a²+b²).
