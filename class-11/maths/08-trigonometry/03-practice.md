# Trigonometric Functions — Practice Range

> **35 practice problems**, graded **Basic (10) · Medium (15) · Advanced (10)**. Each has an **approach hint**
> (open after a real attempt) and a **collapsible full solution**. Topics: radians & ratios, identities, allied
> angles, compound/multiple angles, sum↔product, general solutions, $R$-form.

<!-- RENDERING: inside math use \cdot (not ·), \to, \sum, \frac, \geq/\leq. -->

> **Key formulas:** $\sin^2+\cos^2=1$; $\sin(A\pm B)$, $\cos(A\pm B)$; $\cos2A=1-2\sin^2A$; $\sin\theta=\sin\alpha\Rightarrow n\pi+(-1)^n\alpha$; $\cos\theta=\cos\alpha\Rightarrow 2n\pi\pm\alpha$; $\tan\theta=\tan\alpha\Rightarrow n\pi+\alpha$.

## 🟢 Basic (P1–P10)

### P1
> Convert $120°$ to radians.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$\times \frac{\pi}{180}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$120\cdot\frac{\pi}{180} = \boxed{\tfrac{2\pi}3}$.
</details>

---

### P2
> Find $\cos 30° - \sin 60°$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

Standard values.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\tfrac{\sqrt3}2 - \tfrac{\sqrt3}2 = \boxed{0}$.
</details>

---

### P3

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$\sin\theta = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2\theta}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\sqrt{1 - \tfrac{144}{169}} = \tfrac5{13}$.
</details>

## 🟡 Medium (P11–P25)

### P11
> Find $\tan 15°$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$15° = 45° - 30°$; $\tan(A - B)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\dfrac{1 - \tfrac1{\sqrt3}}{1 + \tfrac1{\sqrt3}} = \dfrac{\sqrt3 - 1}{\sqrt3 + 1} = 2 - \sqrt3$.
</details>

---

### P12
> If $\sin A = \tfrac45$ (acute), find $\cos 2A$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$\cos 2A = 1 - 2\sin^2 A$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$1 - 2\cdot\tfrac{16}{25} = 1 - \tfrac{32}{25} = -\tfrac7{25}$.
</details>

---

### P13
> Simplify $\dfrac{1 + \cos 2\theta}{\sin 2\theta}$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$1 + \cos 2\theta = 2\cos^2\theta$; $\sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\dfrac{2\cos^2\theta}{2\sin\theta\cos\theta} = \cot\theta$.
</details>

---

### P14
> Express $\cos 7x + \cos 3x$ as a product.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$\cos C + \cos D = 2\cos\frac{C+D}2\cos\frac{C-D}2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$2\cos 5x\cos 2x$.
</details>

---

### P15
> Evaluate $\sin 105°$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$105° = 60° + 45°$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\sin 60°\cos 45° + \cos 60°\sin 45° = \tfrac{\sqrt3 + 1}{2\sqrt2} = \tfrac{\sqrt6 + \sqrt2}{4}$.
</details>

---

### P16
> Simplify $\cos(180° + \theta) + \sin(270° - \theta)$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

Allied angles: $\cos(180°+\theta) = -\cos\theta$, $\sin(270°-\theta) = -\cos\theta$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$-\cos\theta - \cos\theta = -2\cos\theta$.
</details>

---

### P17
> Prove $\dfrac{\sin\theta}{1 + \cos\theta} = \tan\dfrac\theta2$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

Half-angle: $\sin\theta = 2\sin\frac\theta2\cos\frac\theta2$, $1 + \cos\theta = 2\cos^2\frac\theta2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\dfrac{2\sin\frac\theta2\cos\frac\theta2}{2\cos^2\frac\theta2} = \tan\tfrac\theta2$. ∎
</details>

---

### P18
> Find the maximum of $\sin\theta + \cos\theta$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$R = \sqrt{1 + 1}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\sqrt2$.
</details>

---

### P19
> Express $2\cos 4x\sin 2x$ as a difference of sines.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$2\cos A\sin B = \sin(A+B) - \sin(A-B)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\sin 6x - \sin 2x$.
</details>

---

### P20
> If $\tan\theta = \tfrac5{12}$, find $\cos 2\theta$ via the $t$-formula.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$\cos 2\theta = \frac{1 - t^2}{1 + t^2}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\dfrac{1 - \tfrac{25}{144}}{1 + \tfrac{25}{144}} = \dfrac{119}{169}$.
</details>

---

### P21
> Solve $\sin\theta = -\tfrac12$ for the principal value in $[-\tfrac\pi2, \tfrac\pi2]$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

Principal value of arcsin.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\theta = -\tfrac\pi6$.
</details>

---

### P22
> Prove $\cos^4\theta - \sin^4\theta = \cos 2\theta$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

Factor as difference of squares.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$(\cos^2 - \sin^2)(\cos^2 + \sin^2) = \cos 2\theta\cdot 1 = \cos 2\theta$. ∎
</details>

---

### P23
> Find $\sin 2\theta$ if $\sin\theta + \cos\theta = \tfrac75$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

Square both sides; $(\sin + \cos)^2 = 1 + \sin 2\theta$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\tfrac{49}{25} = 1 + \sin 2\theta \Rightarrow \sin 2\theta = \tfrac{24}{25}$.
</details>

---

### P24
> Simplify $\tan\theta\cdot\sin 2\theta$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$\sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta$, $\tan = \sin/\cos$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\cdot 2\sin\theta\cos\theta = 2\sin^2\theta$.
</details>

## 🔴 Advanced (P26–P35)

### P26
> Find the general solution of $\cos\theta = -\tfrac12$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$\cos\theta = \cos\frac{2\pi}3$; $\theta = 2n\pi \pm \alpha$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\theta = 2n\pi \pm \tfrac{2\pi}3$.
</details>

---

### P27
> Solve $\cos 3x + \cos x = 0$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

Sum-to-product.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$2\cos 2x\cos x = 0 \Rightarrow \cos 2x = 0$ or $\cos x = 0$. $x = \tfrac{(2n+1)\pi}4$ or $x = \tfrac{(2m+1)\pi}2$.
</details>

---

### P28
> Find the range of $\sin\theta\cos\theta$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$\sin\theta\cos\theta = \tfrac12\sin 2\theta$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\tfrac12\sin 2\theta \in [-\tfrac12, \tfrac12]$.
</details>

---

### P29
> Solve $2\cos^2\theta + \sin\theta - 1 = 0$ (general solution).

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

Replace $\cos^2 = 1 - \sin^2$; quadratic in $\sin\theta$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$2(1 - \sin^2\theta) + \sin\theta - 1 = 0 \Rightarrow 2\sin^2\theta - \sin\theta - 1 = 0 \Rightarrow (2\sin\theta + 1)(\sin\theta - 1) = 0$. $\sin\theta = -\tfrac12 \Rightarrow \theta = n\pi + (-1)^n(-\tfrac\pi6)$; $\sin\theta = 1 \Rightarrow \theta = 2m\pi + \tfrac\pi2$.
</details>

---

### P30
> If $A + B + C = \pi$, prove $\cos 2A + \cos 2B + \cos 2C = -1 - 4\cos A\cos B\cos C$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

Pair two cosines via sum-to-product, use $C = \pi - (A+B)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\cos 2A + \cos 2B = 2\cos(A+B)\cos(A-B) = -2\cos C\cos(A-B)$. Then $\cos 2C = 2\cos^2 C - 1$. Sum $= -1 - 2\cos C[\cos(A-B) - \cos C] = -1 - 2\cos C[\cos(A-B) + \cos(A+B)] = -1 - 4\cos A\cos B\cos C$. ∎
</details>

---

### P31
> Solve $\tan 2x = \cot x$ (general solution).

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$\cot x = \tan(\tfrac\pi2 - x)$; equate tangents.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$2x = n\pi + \tfrac\pi2 - x \Rightarrow 3x = n\pi + \tfrac\pi2 \Rightarrow x = \tfrac{n\pi}3 + \tfrac\pi6$.
</details>

---

### P32
> Find the maximum value of $\sin^4\theta + \cos^4\theta$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$\sin^4 + \cos^4 = 1 - 2\sin^2\theta\cos^2\theta = 1 - \tfrac12\sin^2 2\theta$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

Max when $\sin^2 2\theta = 0$: value $= 1$ (min $= \tfrac12$).
</details>

---

### P33
> Prove $\sin 3\theta = 3\sin\theta - 4\sin^3\theta$.

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

$\sin 3\theta = \sin(2\theta + \theta)$; expand.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$\sin 2\theta\cos\theta + \cos 2\theta\sin\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos^2\theta + (1 - 2\sin^2\theta)\sin\theta = 2\sin\theta(1 - \sin^2\theta) + \sin\theta - 2\sin^3\theta = 3\sin\theta - 4\sin^3\theta$. ∎
</details>

---

### P34
> Solve $\sin\theta + \sin 2\theta + \sin 3\theta = 0$ (general solution).

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

Pair $\sin\theta + \sin 3\theta = 2\sin 2\theta\cos\theta$, factor out $\sin 2\theta$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

$2\sin 2\theta\cos\theta + \sin 2\theta = \sin 2\theta(2\cos\theta + 1) = 0$. $\sin 2\theta = 0 \Rightarrow \theta = \tfrac{n\pi}2$; $\cos\theta = -\tfrac12 \Rightarrow \theta = 2m\pi \pm \tfrac{2\pi}3$.
</details>

---

### P35
> Find the number of solutions of $\cos\theta = \theta$ for $\theta \in [0, \tfrac\pi2]$ (reasoning, not formula).

<details><summary>🧭 Approach</summary>

Compare the curves $y = \cos\theta$ (falling from 1 to 0) and $y = \theta$ (rising from 0); they cross once.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Solution</summary>

At $\theta = 0$: $\cos 0 = 1 > 0$. At $\theta = \tfrac\pi2$: $\cos\tfrac\pi2 = 0 < \tfrac\pi2$. One curve falls, the other rises, so exactly **one** intersection (the Dottie-number region).
</details>
