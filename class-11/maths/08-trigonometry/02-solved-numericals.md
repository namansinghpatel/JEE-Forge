# Trigonometric Functions — Solved Numericals

> **35 worked problems**, split **Basic (10) · Medium (15) · Advanced (10)**, spanning radians & ratios,
> identities, allied angles, compound/multiple angles, sum↔product, general solutions and the $R$-form range.
> Every problem marks the **❓ Question** and **✅ Answer**; Medium and Advanced carry a **🧭 Strategy** block.

<!-- RENDERING: inside math use \cdot (not ·), \to, \sum, \frac, \geq/\leq. -->

> **Key formulas:** $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$; $\sin(A\pm B)=\sin A\cos B\pm\cos A\sin B$; $\cos 2A=1-2\sin^2A$; $\sin\theta=\sin\alpha\Rightarrow\theta=n\pi+(-1)^n\alpha$; $a\cos\theta+b\sin\theta$ has range $[-\sqrt{a^2+b^2},\sqrt{a^2+b^2}]$.

## 🟢 Basic (B1–B10)

<a id="b1"></a>
### B1
> ❓ **Question.** Convert $60°$ to radians.

**✅ Answer.** $60° \times \dfrac{\pi}{180} = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

---

<a id="b2"></a>
### B2
> ❓ **Question.** Convert $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ radians to degrees.

**✅ Answer.** $\dfrac{3\pi}{4} \times \dfrac{180}{\pi} = 135°$.

---

<a id="b3"></a>
### B3
> ❓ **Question.** Find $\sin 30° + \cos 60°$.

**✅ Answer.** $\tfrac12 + \tfrac12 = \boxed{1}$.

---

<a id="b4"></a>
### B4
> ❓ **Question.** If $\sin\theta = \tfrac35$ and $\theta$ is acute, find $\cos\theta$.

**✅ Answer.** $\cos\theta = \sqrt{1 - \tfrac9{25}} = \tfrac45$.

---

<a id="b5"></a>
### B5
> ❓ **Question.** Find $\tan 45° \cdot \cot 45°$.

**✅ Answer.** $1 \cdot 1 = \boxed{1}$.

---

<a id="b6"></a>
### B6
> ❓ **Question.** In which quadrant is $\sin$ positive but $\cos$ negative?

**✅ Answer.** Quadrant II (ASTC: Q2 is "sin +").

---

<a id="b7"></a>
### B7
> ❓ **Question.** Find the arc length of a sector with radius $6$ and angle $\tfrac\pi3$.

**✅ Answer.** $\ell = r\theta = 6\cdot\tfrac\pi3 = 2\pi$.

---

<a id="b8"></a>
### B8
> ❓ **Question.** Simplify $\sin(-\theta) + \cos(-\theta)$.

**✅ Answer.** $-\sin\theta + \cos\theta$ ($\sin$ odd, $\cos$ even).

---

<a id="b9"></a>
### B9
> ❓ **Question.** Find $\sec^2\theta - \tan^2\theta$.

**✅ Answer.** $1$ (Pythagorean identity).

---

<a id="b10"></a>
### B10
> ❓ **Question.** What is the period of $\tan\theta$?

**✅ Answer.** $\pi$.

## 🟡 Medium (M1–M15)

<a id="m1"></a>
### M1
> ❓ **Question.** Find $\sin 75°$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $75° = 45° + 30°$; use $\sin(A+B)$.

**Steps.** $\sin 45°\cos 30° + \cos 45°\sin 30° = \tfrac1{\sqrt2}\cdot\tfrac{\sqrt3}2 + \tfrac1{\sqrt2}\cdot\tfrac12 = \tfrac{\sqrt3 + 1}{2\sqrt2}$.

**✅ Answer.** $\sin 75° = \dfrac{\sqrt3 + 1}{2\sqrt2} = \dfrac{\sqrt6 + \sqrt2}{4}$.

---

<a id="m2"></a>
### M2
> ❓ **Question.** If $\tan A = \tfrac12$ and $\tan B = \tfrac13$, find $\tan(A + B)$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $\tan(A+B) = \frac{\tan A + \tan B}{1 - \tan A\tan B}$.

**Steps.** $\dfrac{\tfrac12 + \tfrac13}{1 - \tfrac16} = \dfrac{5/6}{5/6} = 1$.

**✅ Answer.** $\tan(A+B) = 1$ (so $A + B = 45°$).

---

<a id="m3"></a>
### M3
> ❓ **Question.** If $\sin A = \tfrac35$ (acute), find $\sin 2A$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $\sin 2A = 2\sin A\cos A$; $\cos A = \tfrac45$.

**Steps.** $2\cdot\tfrac35\cdot\tfrac45 = \tfrac{24}{25}$.

**✅ Answer.** $\sin 2A = \tfrac{24}{25}$.

---

<a id="m4"></a>
### M4
> ❓ **Question.** Evaluate $\cos 2A$ if $\sin A = \tfrac35$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $\cos 2A = 1 - 2\sin^2 A$.

**Steps.** $1 - 2\cdot\tfrac9{25} = 1 - \tfrac{18}{25} = \tfrac7{25}$.

**✅ Answer.** $\cos 2A = \tfrac7{25}$.

---

<a id="m5"></a>
### M5
> ❓ **Question.** Simplify $\dfrac{\sin 2\theta}{1 + \cos 2\theta}$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** Use $\sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta$, $1 + \cos 2\theta = 2\cos^2\theta$.

**Steps.** $\dfrac{2\sin\theta\cos\theta}{2\cos^2\theta} = \tan\theta$.

**✅ Answer.** $\tan\theta$.

---

<a id="m6"></a>
### M6
> ❓ **Question.** Express $\sin 5x + \sin x$ as a product.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $\sin C + \sin D = 2\sin\frac{C+D}2\cos\frac{C-D}2$.

**Steps.** $2\sin 3x\cos 2x$.

**✅ Answer.** $2\sin 3x\cos 2x$.

---

<a id="m7"></a>
### M7
> ❓ **Question.** Find $\cos 15°$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $15° = 45° - 30°$; $\cos(A - B)$.

**Steps.** $\cos 45°\cos 30° + \sin 45°\sin 30° = \tfrac1{\sqrt2}\cdot\tfrac{\sqrt3}2 + \tfrac1{\sqrt2}\cdot\tfrac12 = \tfrac{\sqrt3 + 1}{2\sqrt2}$.

**✅ Answer.** $\cos 15° = \dfrac{\sqrt6 + \sqrt2}{4}$.

---

<a id="m8"></a>
### M8
> ❓ **Question.** Prove $\dfrac{1 - \cos\theta}{\sin\theta} = \tan\dfrac\theta2$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $1 - \cos\theta = 2\sin^2\tfrac\theta2$, $\sin\theta = 2\sin\tfrac\theta2\cos\tfrac\theta2$.

**Steps.** $\dfrac{2\sin^2(\theta/2)}{2\sin(\theta/2)\cos(\theta/2)} = \tan\tfrac\theta2$. ∎

**✅ Answer.** Proven.

---

<a id="m9"></a>
### M9
> ❓ **Question.** Simplify $\sin(90° + \theta) + \cos(180° - \theta)$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** Allied angles: $\sin(90°+\theta) = \cos\theta$, $\cos(180°-\theta) = -\cos\theta$.

**Steps.** $\cos\theta - \cos\theta = 0$.

**✅ Answer.** $0$.

---

<a id="m10"></a>
### M10
> ❓ **Question.** If $\cos\theta = -\tfrac12$ and $\theta \in (\tfrac\pi2, \pi)$, find $\theta$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** Reference angle $60°$; cosine is negative in Q2.

**Steps.** $\theta = 180° - 60° = 120° = \tfrac{2\pi}3$.

**✅ Answer.** $\theta = \tfrac{2\pi}3$.

---

<a id="m11"></a>
### M11
> ❓ **Question.** Prove $\tan\theta + \cot\theta = \dfrac{2}{\sin 2\theta}$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** Convert to sin/cos and combine.

**Steps.** $\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} + \dfrac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta} = \dfrac{\sin^2 + \cos^2}{\sin\theta\cos\theta} = \dfrac{1}{\tfrac12\sin 2\theta} = \dfrac2{\sin 2\theta}$. ∎

**✅ Answer.** Proven.

---

<a id="m12"></a>
### M12
> ❓ **Question.** Find the maximum value of $3\sin\theta + 4\cos\theta$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** Max of $a\sin\theta + b\cos\theta$ is $\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$.

**Steps.** $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$.

**✅ Answer.** $5$.

---

<a id="m13"></a>
### M13
> ❓ **Question.** Express $2\sin 3x\cos x$ as a sum.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $2\sin A\cos B = \sin(A+B) + \sin(A-B)$.

**Steps.** $\sin 4x + \sin 2x$.

**✅ Answer.** $\sin 4x + \sin 2x$.

---

<a id="m14"></a>
### M14
> ❓ **Question.** If $\tan\theta = \tfrac{3}{4}$, find $\sin 2\theta$ using the $t$-formula.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $\sin 2\theta = \frac{2t}{1 + t^2}$, $t = \tan\theta$.

**Steps.** $\dfrac{2(3/4)}{1 + 9/16} = \dfrac{3/2}{25/16} = \dfrac{3/2 \cdot 16}{25} = \dfrac{24}{25}$.

**✅ Answer.** $\sin 2\theta = \tfrac{24}{25}$.

---

<a id="m15"></a>
### M15
> ❓ **Question.** Solve $2\cos\theta = 1$ for the principal value.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $\cos\theta = \tfrac12$; principal value.

**Steps.** $\theta = \tfrac\pi3$.

**✅ Answer.** $\theta = \tfrac\pi3$ (principal).

<a id="advanced"></a>
## 🔴 Advanced (A1–A10)

<a id="a1"></a>
### A1
> ❓ **Question.** Find the general solution of $\sin\theta = \tfrac{\sqrt3}2$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $\sin\theta = \sin\alpha \Rightarrow \theta = n\pi + (-1)^n\alpha$; $\alpha = \tfrac\pi3$.

**Steps.** $\theta = n\pi + (-1)^n\tfrac\pi3,\ n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

**✅ Answer.** $\theta = n\pi + (-1)^n\tfrac\pi3$.

---

<a id="a2"></a>
### A2
> ❓ **Question.** Find the general solution of $\cos 2\theta = \tfrac12$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $\cos\phi = \cos\alpha \Rightarrow \phi = 2n\pi \pm \alpha$; here $\phi = 2\theta$, $\alpha = \tfrac\pi3$.

**Steps.** $2\theta = 2n\pi \pm \tfrac\pi3 \Rightarrow \theta = n\pi \pm \tfrac\pi6$.

**✅ Answer.** $\theta = n\pi \pm \tfrac\pi6$.

---

<a id="a3"></a>
### A3
> ❓ **Question.** Solve $\sin 3x + \sin x = 0$ (general solution).

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** Sum-to-product → factor.

**Steps.** $2\sin 2x\cos x = 0 \Rightarrow \sin 2x = 0$ or $\cos x = 0$. $\sin 2x = 0 \Rightarrow x = \tfrac{n\pi}2$; $\cos x = 0 \Rightarrow x = (2m+1)\tfrac\pi2$ (included in $\tfrac{n\pi}2$ for odd $n$).

**✅ Answer.** $x = \tfrac{n\pi}2,\ n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

---

<a id="a4"></a>
### A4
> ❓ **Question.** Find the range of $5\sin\theta - 12\cos\theta$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $R = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$; range $[-R, R]$.

**Steps.** $R = \sqrt{25 + 144} = 13$.

**✅ Answer.** $[-13, 13]$.

---

<a id="a5"></a>
### A5
> ❓ **Question.** If $A + B + C = \pi$, prove $\tan A + \tan B + \tan C = \tan A\tan B\tan C$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $A + B = \pi - C$; take $\tan$ of both sides.

**Steps.** $\tan(A+B) = \tan(\pi - C) = -\tan C$. So $\dfrac{\tan A + \tan B}{1 - \tan A\tan B} = -\tan C \Rightarrow \tan A + \tan B = -\tan C + \tan A\tan B\tan C$, giving $\tan A + \tan B + \tan C = \tan A\tan B\tan C$. ∎

**✅ Answer.** Proven.

---

<a id="a6"></a>
### A6
> ❓ **Question.** Solve $\tan\theta = \sqrt3$ (general solution).

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $\tan\theta = \tan\alpha \Rightarrow \theta = n\pi + \alpha$; $\alpha = \tfrac\pi3$.

**Steps.** $\theta = n\pi + \tfrac\pi3,\ n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

**✅ Answer.** $\theta = n\pi + \tfrac\pi3$.

---

<a id="a7"></a>
### A7
> ❓ **Question.** Prove $\cos 3A = 4\cos^3 A - 3\cos A$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** $\cos 3A = \cos(2A + A)$; expand and use double-angle.

**Steps.** $\cos 2A\cos A - \sin 2A\sin A = (2\cos^2 A - 1)\cos A - 2\sin^2 A\cos A = 2\cos^3 A - \cos A - 2(1 - \cos^2 A)\cos A = 4\cos^3 A - 3\cos A$. ∎

**✅ Answer.** Proven.

---

<a id="a8"></a>
### A8
> ❓ **Question.** Solve $2\sin^2\theta - 3\sin\theta + 1 = 0$ (general solution).

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** Quadratic in $\sin\theta$.

**Steps.** $(2\sin\theta - 1)(\sin\theta - 1) = 0 \Rightarrow \sin\theta = \tfrac12$ or $\sin\theta = 1$. $\sin\theta = \tfrac12 \Rightarrow \theta = n\pi + (-1)^n\tfrac\pi6$; $\sin\theta = 1 \Rightarrow \theta = 2m\pi + \tfrac\pi2$.

**✅ Answer.** $\theta = n\pi + (-1)^n\tfrac\pi6$ or $\theta = 2m\pi + \tfrac\pi2$.

---

<a id="a9"></a>
### A9
> ❓ **Question.** Find the minimum value of $\cos\theta + \cos\left(\theta + \tfrac{2\pi}3\right) + \cos\left(\theta + \tfrac{4\pi}3\right)$.

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** The three cosines are $120°$ apart — they sum to zero identically.

**Steps.** Using sum-to-product or the symmetry of three equally spaced unit vectors, the sum $= 0$ for all $\theta$.

**✅ Answer.** The expression is identically $0$ (min = max = $0$).

---

<a id="a10"></a>
### A10
> ❓ **Question.** Solve $\sqrt3\cos\theta + \sin\theta = 1$ (general solution).

> [!TIP]
> 🧭 **Strategy.** Write LHS as $R\cos(\theta - \phi)$ or divide by $R = 2$.

**Steps.** Divide by $2$: $\tfrac{\sqrt3}2\cos\theta + \tfrac12\sin\theta = \tfrac12 \Rightarrow \cos\left(\theta - \tfrac\pi6\right) = \tfrac12$. So $\theta - \tfrac\pi6 = 2n\pi \pm \tfrac\pi3 \Rightarrow \theta = 2n\pi + \tfrac\pi2$ or $\theta = 2n\pi - \tfrac\pi6$.

**✅ Answer.** $\theta = 2n\pi + \tfrac\pi2$ or $\theta = 2n\pi - \tfrac\pi6,\ n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
