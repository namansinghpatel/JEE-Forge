## 🟢 Basic (P1–P10)

**P1.** Write $K_c$ for $2\text{SO}_2(g) + \text{O}_2(g) \rightleftharpoons 2\text{SO}_3(g)$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Products over reactants, each concentration raised to its coefficient.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K_c = \dfrac{[\text{SO}_3]^2}{[\text{SO}_2]^2[\text{O}_2]}$
</details>

---

**P2.** Find $\Delta n$ for $\text{PCl}_5 \rightleftharpoons \text{PCl}_3 + \text{Cl}_2$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Gas moles of products minus gas moles of reactants.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\Delta n = 2 - 1 = +1$.
</details>

---

**P3.** For $\text{H}_2 + \text{I}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{HI}$, equilibrium concentrations are $[\text{H}_2]=0.5$, $[\text{I}_2]=0.5$, $[\text{HI}]=2.0$. Find $K_c$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Plug directly into $K_c = [\text{HI}]^2/([\text{H}_2][\text{I}_2])$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K_c = \dfrac{2^2}{0.5\times0.5} = \dfrac{4}{0.25} = \mathbf{16}$.
</details>

---

**P4.** A forward reaction has $K = 25$. What is $K$ for the reverse?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Reverse → invert.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K' = 1/25 = \mathbf{0.04}$.
</details>

---

**P5.** For which does $K_p = K_c$: (a) $2\text{NO} + \text{O}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NO}_2$, (b) $\text{H}_2 + \text{Cl}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{HCl}$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$K_p = K_c$ requires $\Delta n = 0$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

(a) $\Delta n = 2-3 = -1$; (b) $\Delta n = 2-2 = 0$ → **(b)**.
</details>

---

**P6.** State the units of $K_c$ for $\text{N}_2 + 3\text{H}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NH}_3$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Units are $(\text{mol/L})^{\Delta n}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\Delta n = 2-4 = -2$, so units $= (\text{mol/L})^{-2} = \mathbf{L^2\,mol^{-2}}$.
</details>

---

**P7.** $K = 12$ and currently $Q = 30$. Which direction?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Compare $Q$ to $K$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$Q > K$ → shifts **backward (←)**.
</details>

---

**P8.** Write $K_p$ for $\text{C}(s) + \text{CO}_2(g) \rightleftharpoons 2\text{CO}(g)$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Leave out pure solid carbon.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K_p = \dfrac{(p_{\text{CO}})^2}{p_{\text{CO}_2}}$.
</details>

---

**P9.** For $A \rightleftharpoons B$, $K = 7$. Find $K$ for $3A \rightleftharpoons 3B$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Coefficients ×3 → $K^3$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K' = 7^3 = \mathbf{343}$.
</details>

---

**P10.** For an exothermic reaction, does $K$ increase or decrease as temperature rises?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Heat is a product; adding heat pushes an exothermic reaction backward.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K$ **decreases** as $T$ rises (equilibrium shifts backward).
</details>

<a id="medium"></a>
## 🟡 Medium (P11–P25)

**P11.** 2 mol $\text{H}_2$ + 2 mol $\text{I}_2$ in a 1 L flask; at equilibrium 1.5 mol $\text{H}_2$ remains. Find $K_c$ for $\text{H}_2 + \text{I}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{HI}$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Change in $\text{H}_2$ = $2 - 1.5 = 0.5$; HI formed = $2\times0.5$. Build the ICE table (1 L → moles are concentrations).
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Eqm: $[\text{H}_2]=[\text{I}_2]=1.5$, $[\text{HI}]=1.0$. $K_c = \dfrac{1^2}{1.5\times1.5} = \dfrac{1}{2.25} = \mathbf{0.44}$.
</details>

---

**P12.** For $\text{N}_2\text{O}_4 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NO}_2$, $K_c = 0.25$ mol/L at 350 K. Find $K_p$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}$, $\Delta n = +1$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K_p = 0.25 \times 0.0821 \times 350 = 0.25 \times 28.7 = \mathbf{7.18\ atm}$.
</details>

---

**P13.** $\text{PCl}_5 \rightleftharpoons \text{PCl}_3 + \text{Cl}_2$ with $\alpha = 0.4$ at total pressure 3 atm. Find $K_p$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Use $K_p = \dfrac{\alpha^2}{1-\alpha^2}P$ ($A \to B + C$ case).
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K_p = \dfrac{0.16}{1-0.16}\times 3 = \dfrac{0.16}{0.84}\times 3 = \mathbf{0.571\ atm}$.
</details>

---

**P14.** For $A \rightleftharpoons B$, $K_c = 9$, start $[A]=1$, $[B]=0$. Find equilibrium concentrations.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$K_c = x/(1-x)$; solve the linear equation.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$x/(1-x)=9 \Rightarrow x = 0.9$. $[A]=0.1$, $[B]=0.9$.
</details>

---

**P15.** $\text{N}_2\text{O}_4 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NO}_2$, 1 mol $\text{N}_2\text{O}_4$ at 1 atm, 50% dissociated. Find $K_p$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$A \to 2B$ case: $K_p = \dfrac{4\alpha^2}{1-\alpha^2}P$, with $\alpha=0.5$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K_p = \dfrac{4(0.25)}{1-0.25}\times1 = \dfrac{1}{0.75} = \mathbf{1.33\ atm}$.
</details>

---

**P16.** Given $A \rightleftharpoons B$ ($K_1=4$) and $B \rightleftharpoons C$ ($K_2=0.5$), find $K$ for $A \rightleftharpoons C$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Adding reactions multiplies constants.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K = 4 \times 0.5 = \mathbf{2}$.
</details>

---

**P17.** At equilibrium a 4 L vessel holds 2 mol $\text{SO}_2$, 2 mol $\text{O}_2$, 4 mol $\text{SO}_3$. Find $K_c$ for $2\text{SO}_2 + \text{O}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{SO}_3$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Divide every mole figure by $V=4$ first.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$[\text{SO}_2]=0.5$, $[\text{O}_2]=0.5$, $[\text{SO}_3]=1.0$. $K_c = \dfrac{1^2}{0.5^2\times0.5} = \dfrac{1}{0.125} = \mathbf{8\ L\,mol^{-1}}$.
</details>

---

**P18.** $\text{N}_2\text{O}_4 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NO}_2$ at 1 atm, $K_p = 0.667$ atm. Find $\alpha$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$K_p = \dfrac{4\alpha^2}{1-\alpha^2}P$; solve for $\alpha^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$0.667 = \dfrac{4\alpha^2}{1-\alpha^2} \Rightarrow 0.667 = 4.667\alpha^2 \Rightarrow \alpha^2 = 0.143 \Rightarrow \alpha = \mathbf{0.378}$.
</details>

---

**P19.** $\text{N}_2 + 3\text{H}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NH}_3$, $K_p = 2$. At one instant $p_{\text{N}_2}=2$, $p_{\text{H}_2}=1$, $p_{\text{NH}_3}=2$ atm. Direction?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Compute $Q_p$ and compare to $K_p$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$Q_p = \dfrac{2^2}{2\times1^3} = \dfrac{4}{2} = 2 = K_p$ → **already at equilibrium**.
</details>

---

**P20.** For $\text{PCl}_5 \rightleftharpoons \text{PCl}_3 + \text{Cl}_2$, $K_c = 2$ mol/L at 600 K. Find $K_p$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\Delta n = +1$; $K_p = K_c(RT)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K_p = 2 \times 0.0821 \times 600 = \mathbf{98.5\ atm}$.
</details>

---

**P21.** Explain, using Le Chatelier, why removing $\text{NH}_3$ as it forms increases the conversion in $\text{N}_2 + 3\text{H}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NH}_3$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Removing a product lowers $Q$ below $K$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Removing product makes $Q < K$, so the system shifts **forward** to replace it, converting more $\text{N}_2$ and $\text{H}_2$. (Industrially $\text{NH}_3$ is condensed out to drive the reaction.)
</details>

---

**P22.** Vapour density of $\text{N}_2\text{O}_4$ falls from a normal $D = 46$ to an observed $d = 30$ ($n=2$). Find $\alpha$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\alpha = \dfrac{D-d}{(n-1)d}$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\alpha = \dfrac{46-30}{30} = \dfrac{16}{30} = \mathbf{0.53}$.
</details>

---

**P23.** $2\text{HI} \rightleftharpoons \text{H}_2 + \text{I}_2$, $K_c = 0.02$. Does increasing total pressure shift this equilibrium? Explain.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Count gas moles on each side.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Moles are equal (2 ↔ 2), so $\Delta n = 0$. **Pressure has no effect** on the position.
</details>

---

**P24.** For $\text{CaCO}_3(s) \rightleftharpoons \text{CaO}(s) + \text{CO}_2(g)$, the equilibrium $\text{CO}_2$ pressure is 0.5 atm. What is $K_p$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Only the gas appears.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K_p = p_{\text{CO}_2} = \mathbf{0.5\ atm}$.
</details>

---

**P25.** An endothermic equilibrium is cooled. State what happens to (a) the position and (b) $K$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Heat is a reactant for an endothermic reaction.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

(a) Shifts **backward** (toward reactants, releasing where heat was absorbed); (b) $K$ **decreases**.
</details>

## 🔴 Advanced (P26–P35)

**P26.** $\text{H}_2 + \text{I}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{HI}$, $K_c = 49$. Start 1 mol each in 1 L. Find equilibrium concentrations.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Symmetric → take the square root of $K_c$ to dodge the quadratic.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\dfrac{2x}{1-x} = 7 \Rightarrow 2x = 7-7x \Rightarrow x = 0.778$. $[\text{HI}]=1.556$, $[\text{H}_2]=[\text{I}_2]=0.222$.
</details>

---

**P27.** $\text{PCl}_5 \rightleftharpoons \text{PCl}_3 + \text{Cl}_2$ at 2 atm has $K_p = 0.5$ atm. Find $\alpha$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$K_p = \dfrac{\alpha^2}{1-\alpha^2}P$; solve for $\alpha^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$0.5 = \dfrac{2\alpha^2}{1-\alpha^2} \Rightarrow 0.5 = 2.5\alpha^2 \Rightarrow \alpha^2 = 0.2 \Rightarrow \alpha = \mathbf{0.447}$.
</details>

---

**P28.** $\text{N}_2\text{O}_4 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NO}_2$, $K_c = 0.36$ M. Start $[\text{N}_2\text{O}_4]=0.2$ M. Find $[\text{NO}_2]$ at equilibrium.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Quadratic: $4x^2 + K_c x - 0.2K_c = 0$; keep the positive root.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$4x^2 + 0.36x - 0.072 = 0 \Rightarrow x = \dfrac{-0.36+\sqrt{0.1296+1.152}}{8} = \dfrac{-0.36+1.132}{8} = 0.0965$. $[\text{NO}_2] = 2x = \mathbf{0.193\ M}$.
</details>

---

**P29.** $2\text{NH}_3 \rightleftharpoons \text{N}_2 + 3\text{H}_2$ at 300 K, $K_p = 8.1\ \text{atm}^2$. Find $K_c$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\Delta n = +2$; $K_c = K_p/(RT)^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$(RT)^2 = (0.0821\times300)^2 = 24.63^2 = 606.6$. $K_c = 8.1/606.6 = \mathbf{0.0134\ (mol/L)^2}$.
</details>

---

**P30.** For $\text{PCl}_5 \rightleftharpoons \text{PCl}_3 + \text{Cl}_2$, $K_p = 0.5$ atm (fixed $T$). Compute $\alpha$ at $P=2$ atm and at $P=0.5$ atm, and state the trend.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Solve $K_p = \dfrac{\alpha^2}{1-\alpha^2}P$ twice; lower $P$ should give larger $\alpha$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$P=2$: $0.5 = 2\alpha^2/(1-\alpha^2) \Rightarrow \alpha^2 = 0.2 \Rightarrow \alpha = 0.447$.
$P=0.5$: $0.5 = 0.5\alpha^2/(1-\alpha^2) \Rightarrow \alpha^2 = 0.5 \Rightarrow \alpha = 0.707$.
Lower pressure → **more dissociation** (0.45 → 0.71), as Le Chatelier predicts.
</details>

---

**P31.** $\text{PCl}_5$: normal vapour density $D = 104.25$, observed $d = 70$ ($n=2$). Find $\alpha$, then $K_p$ at 1 atm.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\alpha = \dfrac{D-d}{d}$, then $K_p = \dfrac{\alpha^2}{1-\alpha^2}P$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\alpha = \dfrac{104.25-70}{70} = \dfrac{34.25}{70} = 0.489$. $K_p = \dfrac{0.239}{1-0.239}\times1 = \dfrac{0.239}{0.761} = \mathbf{0.314\ atm}$.
</details>

---

**P32.** The equilibrium $2\text{SO}_3 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{SO}_2 + \text{O}_2$ is at balance in a rigid vessel; the volume is then **halved**. Using $Q$, predict the shift.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Halving volume doubles every concentration; plug into $Q$ and compare to $K$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Let $K_c = \dfrac{[\text{SO}_2]^2[\text{O}_2]}{[\text{SO}_3]^2}$. Doubling each conc.: $Q = \dfrac{(2s)^2(2o)}{(2t)^2} = \dfrac{8\,s^2 o}{4\,t^2} = 2K$. $Q > K$ → shifts **backward** (toward $\text{SO}_3$, the fewer-moles side) — expected when pressure rises.
</details>

---

**P33.** Given $\text{N}_2 + \text{O}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NO}$ ($K_1 = 4\times10^{-4}$) and $2\text{NO} + \text{O}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NO}_2$ ($K_2 = 6\times10^{10}$), find $K$ for $\text{N}_2 + 2\text{O}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NO}_2$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

The target is the sum of the two given reactions → multiply the constants.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$K = K_1 K_2 = (4\times10^{-4})(6\times10^{10}) = \mathbf{2.4\times10^{7}}$.
</details>

---

**P34.** 3 mol $\text{PCl}_5$ is heated in a 3 L vessel; at equilibrium 1.5 mol has dissociated ($\text{PCl}_5 \rightleftharpoons \text{PCl}_3 + \text{Cl}_2$). Find $K_c$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

ICE in moles, then divide by $V=3$ to get concentrations.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Eqm moles: $\text{PCl}_5 = 1.5$, $\text{PCl}_3 = 1.5$, $\text{Cl}_2 = 1.5$. Concentrations (÷3): all $0.5$ M. $K_c = \dfrac{0.5\times0.5}{0.5} = \mathbf{0.5\ mol/L}$.
</details>

---

**P35.** For $\text{N}_2 + 3\text{H}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NH}_3$ an equilibrium is set up from 1 mol $\text{N}_2$ + 3 mol $\text{H}_2$ in a 1 L vessel; at equilibrium 50% of the $\text{N}_2$ has reacted. Find $K_c$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\text{N}_2$ reacted $= 0.5$ mol; use stoichiometry (3:1 for $\text{H}_2$, 2:1 for $\text{NH}_3$) for the others. 1 L so moles = concentrations.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Eqm: $[\text{N}_2]=0.5$, $[\text{H}_2]=3-1.5=1.5$, $[\text{NH}_3]=1.0$. $K_c = \dfrac{1^2}{0.5\times1.5^3} = \dfrac{1}{1.6875} = \mathbf{0.59\ L^2\,mol^{-2}}$.
</details>
