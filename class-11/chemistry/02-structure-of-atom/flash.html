Bohr & Spectra

1Photon energy

\(E=h\nu=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}\)
\(E(\text{eV})=1240/\lambda(\text{nm})\).

2Bohr energy

\(E_n=-13.6\dfrac{Z^2}{n^2}\ \text{eV}\)
Negative; 0 at \(n=\infty\).

3Bohr radius

\(r_n=0.529\dfrac{n^2}{Z}\ \text{Å}\)

4Rydberg

\(\bar\nu=R_HZ^2\!\left(\dfrac1{n_1^2}-\dfrac1{n_2^2}\right)\)
Lyman(1) UV · Balmer(2) visible.

5Lines to ground

\(\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}\)

Dual Nature

6de Broglie

\(\lambda=\dfrac{h}{mv}=\dfrac hp\)
Lighter → longer λ.

7Photoelectric

\(h\nu=W_0+KE_{max}\)
Frequency, not brightness.

8Uncertainty

\(\Delta x\,\Delta p\ge\dfrac{h}{4\pi}\)

9Accelerated e⁻

\(\lambda=\dfrac{12.27}{\sqrt V}\ \text{Å}\)
\(V\) in volts.

Quantum Model

10Quantum numbers

\(n\) shell · \(l\) \(0..n{-}1\) · \(m_l\) \(-l..+l\) · \(m_s=\pm\tfrac12\).

11Capacities

\(2n^2\) shell, \(2(2l+1)\) subshell

12Nodes

\(n-1\) total; \(l\) angular
radial \(=n-l-1\).

13Filling rules

Aufbau · Pauli · Hund. Order by \(n+l\).

14Exceptions

Cr \([Ar]3d^54s^1\); Cu \([Ar]3d^{10}4s^1\).

Advanced & Traps

15Scale any ion

H×: energy \(\times Z^2\), radius \(\times 1/Z\), velocity \(\times Z\). He⁺ IE = 54.4 eV.

16Ions: 4s before 3d

Fe²⁺ = \([Ar]3d^6\); Fe³⁺ = \([Ar]3d^5\).

17Common traps

Bohr only for 1-electron species; energies negative; node total \(=n-1\).
