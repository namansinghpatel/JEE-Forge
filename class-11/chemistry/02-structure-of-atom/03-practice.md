> structure, Bohr's model, spectra, dual nature, quantum numbers and configuration. Each has a **💡 Approach
> hint** (*how to think* — reveal only if stuck) and a **collapsible answer**. Attempt with pen and paper first.

## 🟢 Basic (P1–P10)

**P1.** How many neutrons are in ${}^{35}_{17}\text{Cl}$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

neutrons $= A - Z$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$35 - 17 = \mathbf{18}$.
</details>

---

**P2.** Classify ${}^{40}_{18}\text{Ar}$, ${}^{40}_{19}\text{K}$, ${}^{40}_{20}\text{Ca}$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Same $A$, different $Z$?
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

Same mass number → **isobars**.
</details>

---

**P3.** Find the wavelength of light with frequency $6\times10^{14}\ \mathrm{Hz}$.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\lambda = c/\nu$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\lambda = \dfrac{3\times10^8}{6\times10^{14}} = 5\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{m} = \mathbf{500\ nm}$.
</details>

---

**P4.** Energy of a photon of wavelength $620\ \mathrm{nm}$ (in eV)?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$E = 1240/\lambda$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$1240/620 = \mathbf{2\ eV}$.
</details>

---

**P5.** Radius of the 3rd Bohr orbit of hydrogen?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$r = 0.529\,n^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$0.529(9) = \mathbf{4.76\ Å}$.
</details>

---

**P6.** Energy of the $n=2$ level of hydrogen?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$E_n = -13.6/n^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$-13.6/4 = \mathbf{-3.4\ eV}$.
</details>

---

**P7.** Maximum electrons in the $n=3$ shell?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$2n^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$2(9) = \mathbf{18}$.
</details>

---

**P8.** How many orbitals in the $2p$ subshell?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$2l+1$ with $l=1$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\mathbf{3}$.
</details>

---

**P9.** Configuration of oxygen ($Z=8$)?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Fill $1s,2s,2p$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^4$.
</details>

---

**P10.** Number of unpaired electrons in nitrogen ($Z=7$)?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$2p^3$, Hund's rule.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\mathbf{3}$ (each $2p$ singly filled).
</details>

<a id="medium"></a>
## 🟡 Medium (P11–P25)

**P11.** Energy released in the $n=4\to2$ transition of hydrogen?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\Delta E = 13.6(1/4 - 1/16)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$13.6(0.25 - 0.0625) = 13.6(0.1875) = \mathbf{2.55\ eV}$.
</details>

---

**P12.** Wavelength of the $n=2\to1$ line of hydrogen?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\Delta E = 10.2$ eV; $\lambda = 1240/E$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$1240/10.2 \approx \mathbf{121.6\ nm}$.
</details>

---

**P13.** Energy of the ground state of Li²⁺ ($Z=3$)?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$E = -13.6\,Z^2/n^2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$-13.6(9)/1 = \mathbf{-122.4\ eV}$.
</details>

---

**P14.** de Broglie wavelength of a proton ($m = 1.67\times10^{-27}$ kg) at $10^5\ \mathrm{m/s}$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\lambda = h/(mv)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\dfrac{6.626\times10^{-34}}{1.67\times10^{-27}\times10^5} \approx \mathbf{3.97\times10^{-12}\ m}$.
</details>

---

**P15.** A metal's work function is $2.5\ \mathrm{eV}$. Threshold wavelength?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\lambda_0 = 1240/W_0$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$1240/2.5 = \mathbf{496\ nm}$.
</details>

---

**P16.** Which is not allowed: $n=3,l=0,m_l=0$; $n=3,l=3,m_l=0$; $n=4,l=2,m_l=-1$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$l \le n-1$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$n=3, l=3$ is invalid ($l$ max $= 2$).
</details>

---

**P17.** How many electrons can have $n=4, l=1$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$4p$ subshell → $2(2l+1)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$2(3) = \mathbf{6}$.
</details>

---

**P18.** Configuration of copper ($Z=29$)?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Fully-filled exception.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$[\text{Ar}]\,3d^{10}\,4s^1$.
</details>

---

**P19.** Configuration of Mn²⁺ ($Z$ of Mn $=25$)?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

Mn $= [\text{Ar}]3d^5 4s^2$; remove 4s first.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$[\text{Ar}]\,3d^5$ (5 unpaired electrons).
</details>

---

**P20.** Number of spectral lines from $n=5$ to ground?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$n(n-1)/2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$5(4)/2 = \mathbf{10}$.
</details>

---

**P21.** Velocity of the electron in the 1st Bohr orbit of He⁺ ($Z=2$)?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$v_n = 2.18\times10^6\,Z/n$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$2.18\times10^6(2)/1 = \mathbf{4.36\times10^6\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P22.** Wavenumber of the series limit of the Balmer series?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\bar\nu = R_H(1/4 - 0)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$1.097\times10^7/4 \approx \mathbf{2.74\times10^6\ m^{-1}}$ ($\lambda \approx 365$ nm).
</details>

---

**P23.** Uncertainty in velocity of an electron confined to $\Delta x = 0.5\ \text{Å}$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\Delta v = h/(4\pi m\Delta x)$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\dfrac{6.626\times10^{-34}}{4\pi(9.1\times10^{-31})(0.5\times10^{-10})} \approx \mathbf{1.16\times10^6\ m/s}$.
</details>

---

**P24.** Number of unpaired electrons in Cr ($Z=24$)?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$[\text{Ar}]3d^5 4s^1$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$5 + 1 = \mathbf{6}$ unpaired.
</details>

---

**P25.** Total number of nodes in a $3p$ orbital?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

total $= n-1$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$3-1 = \mathbf{2}$ (1 radial + 1 angular).
</details>

<a id="advanced"></a>
## 🔴 Advanced (P26–P35)

**P26.** Shortest wavelength of the Balmer series of hydrogen?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$n_2\to\infty$, $n_1=2$: $\bar\nu = R_H/4$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\lambda = 4/R_H = 4/(1.097\times10^7) \approx \mathbf{365\ nm}$.
</details>

---

**P27.** Radial and angular nodes of a $5f$ orbital?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

radial $= n-l-1$, angular $= l$, with $l=3$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

radial $= 5-3-1 = 1$; angular $= 3$; total $= 4$.
</details>

---

**P28.** Ionisation energy of Li²⁺ from the ground state?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$13.6\,Z^2$ with $Z=3$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$13.6(9) = \mathbf{122.4\ eV}$.
</details>

---

**P29.** de Broglie wavelength of an electron accelerated through $400\ \mathrm{V}$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\lambda = 12.27/\sqrt{V}$ Å.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$12.27/20 \approx \mathbf{0.61\ Å}$.
</details>

---

**P30.** Photon of $150\ \mathrm{nm}$ hits a metal of work function $5\ \mathrm{eV}$. Find the stopping potential.

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$E = 1240/150$; $V_0 = (E - W_0)$ in volts.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$E = 8.27\ \mathrm{eV}$; $V_0 = 8.27 - 5 = \mathbf{3.27\ V}$.
</details>

---

**P31.** Energy to ionise a hydrogen electron already in $n=2$?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$IE = 0 - E_2 = 13.6/4$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\mathbf{3.4\ eV}$.
</details>

---

**P32.** Four quantum numbers of the valence electron of sodium ($Z=11$)?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$[\text{Ne}]3s^1$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$(3, 0, 0, +\tfrac12)$.
</details>

---

**P33.** Ratio of radii of the 3rd orbit of hydrogen to the 1st orbit of He⁺?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$r \propto n^2/Z$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$\dfrac{9/1}{1/2} = \mathbf{18}$.
</details>

---

**P34.** An electron and a proton have the same KE. Which has the longer de Broglie wavelength?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\lambda = h/\sqrt{2mKE}$ — larger $m$ → shorter $\lambda$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

The **electron** (smaller mass → longer wavelength).
</details>

---

**P35.** The first excitation energy of He⁺ ($n=1\to2$)?

<details><summary>💡 Approach hint</summary>

$\Delta E = 13.6\,Z^2(1 - 1/4)$ with $Z=2$.
</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer</summary>

$13.6(4)(0.75) = \mathbf{40.8\ eV}$.
</details>
