## The mole bridge

$$n = \frac{m}{M}, \qquad N = n\,N_A, \qquad V_{gas} = n\times 22.4\ \text{L (STP)}$$

Always convert to **moles** first; balanced-equation ratios are in moles.

---

## Avogadro and molar mass

$$N_A = 6.022\times10^{23}\ \text{mol}^{-1}$$

- Molar mass $M$ (g/mol) = atomic/molecular/formula mass in u.
- Average atomic mass $= \sum(\text{fractional abundance}\times\text{isotope mass})$.
- $1\ \text{mol O}_2 = N_A$ molecules $= 2N_A$ atoms; $1\ \text{mol CaCl}_2 = 3N_A$ ions.

---

## Formulae

$$\%\,\text{element} = \frac{(\text{atoms})\times(\text{atomic mass})}{\text{molar mass}}\times100$$
$$\text{Molecular formula} = (\text{Empirical})\times n, \qquad n = \frac{\text{molar mass}}{\text{empirical formula mass}}$$

Empirical formula steps: % → grams (per 100 g) → ÷ atomic mass → ÷ smallest → whole numbers.

---

## Stoichiometry

- Balanced equation coefficients = **mole ratios**.
- **Limiting reagent:** smallest value of (moles ÷ coefficient). Base all products and leftovers on it.
- $$\%\,\text{yield} = \frac{\text{actual}}{\text{theoretical}}\times100, \qquad \%\,\text{purity} = \frac{\text{pure mass}}{\text{sample mass}}\times100$$

## Concentration

| Term | Formula | Denominator |
|---|---|---|
| Molarity $M$ | moles solute / L solution | volume of solution (T-dependent) |
| Molality $m$ | moles solute / kg solvent | mass of solvent |
| Mole fraction $x$ | moles component / total moles | total moles |
| Mass % | mass solute / mass solution × 100 | mass of solution |
| ppm | mass solute / mass solution × $10^6$ | mass of solution |

$$\text{Dilution: } M_1V_1 = M_2V_2 \qquad\qquad \text{From density: } M = \frac{10\,d\,(\text{mass }\%)}{\text{molar mass}}$$
