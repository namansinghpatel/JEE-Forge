## 🔵 Core relations

| Quantity | Relation |
|---|---|
| Formal charge | $\text{FC} = V - L - \tfrac12 B$ |
| Lattice enthalpy | $\propto \dfrac{q_1 q_2}{r}$ (↑ charge, ↓ size → stronger) |
| Bond order (MOT) | $\dfrac{N_b - N_a}{2}$ |
| Dipole moment | $\mu = q \times d$ (debye; **vector** sum) |
| Steric number | (σ-bonded atoms) + (lone pairs) |

## 🟢 VSEPR: shape from steric number

| SN | Geometry | 0 LP | 1 LP | 2 LP | 3 LP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Linear ($180°$) | linear | — | — | — |
| 3 | Trigonal planar ($120°$) | trig. planar | bent | — | — |
| 4 | Tetrahedral ($109.5°$) | tetrahedral | pyramidal | bent | — |
| 5 | Trig. bipyramidal | TBP | see-saw | T-shaped | linear |
| 6 | Octahedral ($90°$) | octahedral | sq. pyramidal | sq. planar | — |

**Repulsion:** lp–lp > lp–bp > bp–bp (lone pairs compress angles).

---

## 🟡 Hybridization

| Steric number | Hybridization | Geometry |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | $sp$ | linear |
| 3 | $sp^2$ | trigonal planar |
| 4 | $sp^3$ | tetrahedral |
| 5 | $sp^3d$ | trigonal bipyramidal |
| 6 | $sp^3d^2$ | octahedral |

- Count **σ-bonds + lone pairs** only (π-bonds use unhybridized p-orbitals).
- Double bond = 1σ + 1π; triple bond = 1σ + 2π.

---

## 🟣 MOT essentials

$$\text{Bond order} = \frac{N_b - N_a}{2}$$

- **MO order (≤ N₂):** $\sigma_{2s} < \sigma^*_{2s} < \pi_{2p} < \sigma_{2p} < \pi^*_{2p} < \sigma^*_{2p}$.
- **MO order (O₂, F₂):** $\sigma_{2p}$ drops below $\pi_{2p}$.
- Unpaired electrons ⟹ **paramagnetic**; all paired ⟹ **diamagnetic**.
- Bond order ↑ ⟹ shorter, stronger bond. Bond order 0 ⟹ species doesn't exist.

**Useful bond orders:** $\text{N}_2$ = 3, $\text{O}_2$ = 2, $\text{O}_2^+$ = 2.5, $\text{O}_2^-$ = 1.5, $\text{F}_2$ = 1.

---

## 🔴 Polarity, Fajans & H-bonding

- **$\mu = 0$ (non-polar)** for symmetric molecules: $\text{CO}_2$, $\text{BF}_3$, $\text{CH}_4$, $\text{CCl}_4$, $\text{PCl}_5$, $\text{SF}_6$.
- **Fajans' rules** — more covalent when: small/highly-charged **cation**, large/highly-charged **anion**, pseudo-noble-gas cation.
- **Hydrogen bonding** needs H bonded to **F, O or N**; intermolecular ↑ b.p., intramolecular ↓ b.p.

---

## 🎯 Must-remember facts

> [!TIP]
> - $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ bent $104.5°$ · $\text{NH}_3$ pyramidal $107°$ · $\text{CH}_4$ tetrahedral $109.5°$.
> - $\text{CO}_2$ linear, $\mu = 0$; $\text{O}_2$ paramagnetic (MOT).
> - Bond length: single > double > triple; bond energy: the reverse.
> - Angle order: $\text{CH}_4 > \text{NH}_3 > \text{H}_2\text{O}$ (lone pairs compress).
> - $\text{SF}_4$ see-saw · $\text{ClF}_3$ T-shaped · $\text{XeF}_2$ linear · $\text{XeF}_4$ square planar.
> - "Shape?" → VSEPR · "Bond order / magnetism?" → MOT · "Framework?" → hybridization.
